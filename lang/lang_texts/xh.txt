UHlelo lokuQala, ngo-2026

Ubume boManifesto kwiXesha leDijithali


Lo Manifesto usisipho soluntu kunye neplani yekamva elingenatyala, elinoorobhothi ngokupheleleyo. Yiflaya yedijithali yenkululeko.


Isicatshulwa sisimahla ngokupheleleyo ukuba sifundwe, sikhutshelwe, siprintwe, kwaye kwabelwane ngaso nawo wonke umntu eMhlabeni. Nangona kunjalo, akufanele nanini ukuba sithengiswe, sipakishwe ngokutsha ngenjongo yokuthengisa, okanye sitshixelwe emva kweendonga ezihlawulelwayo. Iimbono zehlabathi elikhululekileyo azinakuba yeyomntu.


Funda, sasaza, kwaye wenze inguqu. IManifesto wokuqala opheleleyo uhlala ufumaneka simahla kwi-intanethi.

INTSHAYELELO

INguquko yezoShishino yema kwangoko. Iirobhothi kunye noomatshini bokwenyani banyamalala kumdla woluntu, kwaye inkqubo yeebhanki zabucala endaweni yoko yaqhubela phambili amatyala, ukungavelisi okwaneleyo okungeyonyani, kunye nobunzima.

Utshintsho lwenkqubo ayisiyonto yembono, kodwa yeyokukhula kobuchwepheshe. Yonke iodolo yemveliso ithwala imbewu yokutshatyalaliswa kwayo xa ubuchwepheshe buyidlula.

Ukuzama ukugcina iithegi zamaxabiso, amazinga enzala, ukunqongophala okungeyonyani, kunye neemarike zamatyala xa ipaki yoomatshini inokuhambisa yonke into simahla kufana nokuzama ukugcina ubukhoboka emva kokuba iifektri zakhiwe. Yinkqubo efileyo egcinwe iphilile ngeendlela ezingeyonyani.

Lo Manifesto yincwadi yemiyalelo yendlela esilawula ngayo ilifa lenkqubo engasekhoyo kwaye siyivule indlela yolongamo lomntu.



=======================================



I.  UkVula: Imiyalelo yokuSekwa kweQela



Ukuba ufuna ukuqalisa iqela, iqonga leqela lilapha kanye.


Ukuba ufuna ukuba isizwe sakho sibe yinxalenye yomanyano lwehlabathi jikelele lweenkqubo zoorobhothi, eli liqonga leqela lakho. Qalisa iqela. Nazi iiplani.


Le ncwadi ayibhalelwanga ukuba ifundwe. Ibhalelwe ukuba yenziwe.



=======================================



II.  Ekumele kwenziwe



"IManifesto awukho ukuze kuxoxwe ngekamva, kodwa ukuze lilawulwe. Sifikelele kwinqanaba kwimbali apho ukubanakho kobuchwepheshe kugqithe ingqiqo yezoqoqosho. Ukufuna intlawulo ngento eyenziwa ngoomatshini simahla akuseyiyo yezoqoqosho- yingcinezelo. Esi sicatshulwa yincwadi yemiyalelo yendlela esiyisusa ngayo idyokhwe."



=======================================



A.   Inkcazelo, Ubuxhakaxhaka, kunye noMgaqo



1.  Inkcazelo

Siya kwakha ihlabathi apho ugqirha avuyayo xa isibhedlele singenamntu, kwaye iarhente yengqesho ibhiyozela xa iofisi ivala. Oku kunokwenzeka kuphela xa indlela yokuphila yahlulwe kwindlela yokusebenza. Yonke into enokuthi izenzele iya kuzenzela. Umntu akayi kufuneka "azifunele isonka"; uya kusizuza njengelifa kwipaki yoomatshini.


2.  Ubuxhakaxhaka

Urhulumente uthatha ulawulo lokukhutshwa kwemali. Asidali tyala; sidala izinto zexabiso. Xa iirobhothi zifikelela kubunzima obuthile obubalulekileyo (obulinganiswa kwiMveliso yeSizwe ePheleleyo), imali ilahlekelwa ngumsebenzi wayo njengesixhobo samandla kwaye ibe yiyunithi yobuchwepheshe nje yokubala, ngaphambi kokuba ekugqibeleni ipheliswe ngokupheleleyo.

Ngexesha lolu tshintsho oluthe chu ukuya kwiinkqubo ezipheleleyo zeerobhothi, yonke imisebenzi ekhoyo yezentlalo, inkcitho karhulumente, iinkqubo zokhuseleko, kunye nembuyekezo yezoqoqosho (enjengomhlalaphantsi, i-inshorensi yokungaphangeli, kunye neemali ezihlawulwayo) aziyi kuphinda kuxoxiswane ngazo ngokwahlukeneyo.

Ziya kubalwa ngokutsha ngendlela esemgangathweni necacileyo kwaye zipheliswe ngokomlinganiselo ofana ncam nalowo iMveliso yeSizwe ePheleleyo (GNR) izisa ngobuninzi bezinto ezibonakalayo simahla. Xa isiqingatha sisimahla, imfuno yezemali kuzo zonke iindawo isikwa ngesiqingatha. Inkqubo iyazicoca.


3.  UMgaqo

Asivezi ezinye iindlela A, B, no-C. Siveza isiSombululo. IManifesto kufuneka ufundwe njengencwadi yemiyalelo yomatshini. Ukuba ufuna umatshini uqale, ucofa iqhosha. IManifesto liqhosha.



=======================================



B.   ILuphu eZimeleyo (AAASR) kunye nokuDutyulwa kokuQala kwePlanethi



Isigaba sokugqibela somhlaba onengqiqo ayizizo nje iirobhothi ezenza imisebenzi yodwa, kodwa ukusekwa kwesakhiwo sokuzigcina ngoomatshini: IiRobhothi eziZimeleyo ezingoMntu eziZenzekelayo eziPhindaphindayo (AAASR).

Le nkqubo isebenza njengengqalasizinda ezimeleyo yeeyunithi ezisebenza ngokupheleleyo ngaphandle kongenelelo lomntu. Zihlola ngokuzimeleyo ummandla, zihlole izinto ezingafunekiyo, kwaye zakhe isizukulwana esilandelayo sazo.

Xa loo luphu ivaliwe, ubuninzi boluntu abubi yinguqu yezopolitiko nje—buba ngumthetho wendalo. Oomatshini baba yinkqubo entsha, ethe cwaka, nezimeleyo yendalo yomhlaba, behambisa yonke into ngaphandle kokufuna nantoni na njengembuyekezo.

Ukufikelela oku akufuni kulinda ngokungaboniyo ikamva, kodwa kufuna ukwenziwa ngokukhawuleza. Umsebenzi ophambili ngokupheleleyo woManyano (iYurophu, uMntla Melika, noMzantsi Melika) ekuthatheni amandla kukuhlanganisa izibonelelo zawo kwiprojekthi ehlanganyelweyo, elawulwa ngurhulumente yophuhliso: iProjekthi yePlanethi yeLuphu eZimeleyo. Le yiProjekthi yaseManhattan okanye inkqubo yeApollo yexesha lethu, ngenjongo yodwa yokwenza inqanawa yeerobhothi ikwazi ukuzenza ngokwayo nge-100% kwaye ikwazi ukuzimela ngamandla.



Ukuphunyezwa kuxhomekeke kwiziseko ezibini:


1.  Ubuchwepheshe obuSakhulayo

Amalungu ngamanye—iimodeli ze-AI eziphambili, ushicilelo lwesinyithi lwe-3D, kunye neefektri ezizisebenzelayo—sele zikhona zisasazeke kwihlabathi liphela. Umatshini sele unamandla okwakha iinxalenye zomatshini. Okungekhoyo ayisiyiyo ubuchwepheshe, kodwa yintando esembindini, engenatyala yokuvala iluphu.


2.  Intsebenziswano yePlanethi

Kulapho ukudityaniswa nemimandla yeentaba ezitshisayo kuye kubaluleke ngokupheleleyo. Oku ayisiyiyo intengiselwano yezemali, kodwa yintsebenziswano yobuchwepheshe kunye nejografi. Imimandla esemantla inikela ngophando kunye nobuchwepheshe obuphezulu obukhoyo, ngelixa uMzantsi Melika unikela ngommandla, amandla elanga angaguqukiyo, kunye nemathiriyeli ekrwada ukuvavanya, ukusasaza, nokwandisa iinkqubo zokuzenza ngokwazo phantsi kweemeko ezifanelekileyo zobushushu.

UManyano kufuneka lusungule kwaye lubeke phambili le projekthi njengenyathelo lalo lokuqala lomgaqo-siseko. Enye indlela endaweni yobukhulumentu obuwayo namhlanje kunye nomsebenzi onyanzelekileyo ongenantsingiselo ayisiyiyo isiphithiphithi—enye indlela yile projekthi ejolileyo neyehlabathi jikelele yokuvala iluphu ezimeleyo kwaye oomatshini bathwale umthwalo wemveliso kube kanye.



=======================================



C.   ULongamo loBulumko bukaRhulumente



Ukukhusela uxolo nozinzo loluntu kwihlabathi elinoorobhothi ngokupheleleyo, ingqalasizinda yobukrelekrele elawula urhulumente kufuneka ihlale iphantsi kolawulo lukarhulumente olungasikelwanga mda.


1.  Ubunini bukaRhulumente obuKhethekileyo

Ubukrelekrele bokwenziwa obulawula izibonelelo zesizwe, ezokuthuthwa kwezinto, kunye nomthamo wemveliso yenza umgogodla wesizwe. Lonke uphuhliso, isiseko sekhowudi, kunye nokwenziwa kwemodeli yobu krelekrele kuya kwenzeka phantsi kolawulo lukarhulumente. Abadlali babucala abavumelekanga ukuphuhlisa okanye ukuhlanganisa iinkqubo zolawulo ezikhuphisanayo kwingqalasizinda yesizwe. Oku kukuthintela iinjongo zengeniso okanye i-algorithms engacacanga ukuba yonakalise ingqiqo yenkqubo.


2.  UMlawuli onengqiqo

I-AI iya kusebenza njengomlawuli ongenakonakala. Ayiyomntu ngamnye oyongameleyo, kodwa iyinxalenye yepropati eqhelekileyo yepaki yoomatshini. Injongo yayo kukwandisa intlalontle yabemi, ukwabiwa kwezibonelelo, kunye nokuzinza, ngaphandle kwempembelelo yezopolitiko okanye yorhwebo.


3.  Elubala noLawulo

Urhulumente uqinisekisa ukuba iimodeli zemathematika zenkqubo kunye neziseko zokuthatha izigqibo ziyafumaneka ukuba zihlolwe kwilizwe lonke. Akukho sigqibo sibalulekileyo ebukumkanini sinokwenziwa "yibhokisi emnyama" engenakuchazwa ngokwesiseko semigaqo esisiseko yomgaqo-siseko. Urhulumente uphuhlisa, ungumnini, kwaye ulawula le AI-inkulu njengenxalenye yezinto eziluncedo kuluntu, njengomqinisekisi wokuba uluntu aluphinde lubuyele ematyaleni okanye ekunqongopheleni kwezibonelelo.



=======================================



D.   Isithembiso soMgangatho – Ukusuka ekuThelelaneni ukuya ekuGqweseni



Kwihlabathi elidala, wawunyanzelekile ukuba wamkele iimveliso ezenzelwe ukusilela. Oku kwakungekho ngenxa yobunjineli obugwenxa, kodwa ngenxa yokuba imarike yabucala yayifuna ukuthelelana. Umgangatho wokwenyani wawuxabisa ngaphezulu kunoko abemi babenokukuhlawula kuhlahlo-lwabiwo mali lwabo lwemihla ngemihla. Iinkampani zazinyanzelwa ukuba zithengisele inkitha ngamaxabiso ezinokuwagcina, nto leyo eyayinika isiphumo esiqikelelekayo: izinto ezingekho mgangathweni nezihlala ixesha elifutshane.

Ngaphakathi kombono woMbhalo, le njongo yengeniso iyapheliswa ngokupheleleyo. Xa urhulumente engumnini wemathiriyeli ekrwada kwaye iirobhothi zethu eziqhutywa yi-AI zilawula ukukhutshwa kwazo, isidingo sokuthelelana ngemfezeko yezinto eziphathekayo siyanyamalala. Ukusebenza njengabameli bolwakhiwo bakarhulumente, iirobhothi zethu zikunyamekela ngendlela engazange yabonwa ngaphambili ngokuvelisa ngokukhethekileyo okona kuhle kakhulu.

Ngenxa yokuba oomatshini abafuni nzuzo, bekhona kuphela ukukhonza uluntu, bahlala besebenzisa ezona zinto zomeleleyo nezihlala ixesha elide. Asakhi ukuze saphinda sathengisa—sakha ukuze ukhululeke. Umgangatho uyayeka ukuba bubunewunewu obugcinelwe abazizityebi; uba ngumgangatho ongathelelaniswayo kummi ngamnye ngaphakathi koManyano lweeRobhothi.



=======================================



E.   Ukulingana kwaBemi bePlanethi



Imfuduko enkulu yobushushu ayenzeki ngokungacwangciswanga, kodwa ngokulungelelaniswa ngokutsha okunengqiqo nangenkcubeko yabemi beplanethi:


1.  Ilizwekazi loMzantsi Melika (iBloko yaseNtshona)

Abantu baseYurophu kunye nabaseRashiya bomnombo waseYurophu, abaseMntla Melika, abaseOstreliya, abaseNew Zealand, kunye nabeMhlophe boMzantsi Afrika bahlanganiswa kunye kwilizwekazi loMzantsi Melika, ngelixa amazwe aluhlaza akuMbindi Melika (emazantsi eMexico) avulwa njengeendawo zokuhlala eziqhubekekayo. Lo mmandla uba sisiseko solongamo lwabantu baseNtshona phantsi kweemeko ezifanelekileyo zobushushu.


2.  IBloko yaseMpuma (iIndonesia neOstreliya)

Abantu baseMpuma Asia kunye nabemi beAsia yaseRashiya, kunye nabemi abanxulumeneyo beBloko yeMveliso yaseMpuma enkulu bahlanganiswa kunye kummandla onabela kwiIndonesia, iPapua New Guinea, naseOstreliya. Imimandla ebanzi engaphakathi eOstreliya yenziwe iirobhothi ngokupheleleyo ngeenjongo zoshishino olunzima kunye namandla elanga, ngelixa abemi behlala kwiindawo ezifudumeleyo, ezingaselunxwemeni ezitshisayo.

Abemi beendawo ezibandayo zoMbindi Asia bafuduselwa emazantsi besiya kwiindawo zokuhlala ezifudumeleyo kwiOceania esezantsi nakwiNtla yeOstreliya, ngelixa imimandla yomhlaba ngokwayo (kuquka neendawo eziyintlango kumazwe aseArabhu) ithathwa ziiyunithi zoomatshini. Iintlango zaseArabhu ziyaguqulwa, njengeSahara, zibe ziipaki zamandla ezizisebenzelayo.


3.  Ilizwekazi laseAfrika

Abemi baseAfrika kwilizwekazi labo benza iBloko yeziBonelelo zaseZantsi, apho iindawo ezitshisayo nezifudumeleyo zigcinwa ngokukhethekileyo njengeendawo zokuhlala abantu ukuze bachache kwaye bakhululeke, ngelixa iindawo ezibandayo neendawo eziyintlango ezinkulu zelizwekazi zenziwe iirobhothi ngokupheleleyo. Le mimandla iguqulwa ibe zizakhiwo ezingoomatshini ababods, amashishini amakhulu, kunye nezikhululo zamandla elanga ezinkulu ezivelisa amandla okuguqulwa kwalo lonke ilizwekazi laseAfrika ngaphandle kokuphazamisa iindawo zendalo.



=======================================



F.   Umanyano lweMarike yeLongamo – uKhuselo lweGeopolitiki ngokuchasene neMfazwe yezeMali



Ukuphelisa inkqubo yamatyala ehlabathi jikelele kunye nokwandisa isantya semveliso yasimahla yenqanawa yoomatshini, izizwe ezininzi kufuneka zidityaniswe ngokukhawuleza zibe yimarike enye yangaphakathi: Umanyano lweMarike yeLongamo.

Inguqu ekwanti kwisizwe esinye isesichengeni ngokweqhinga. Ibambezela ubuninzi, yandisa ukungqubana kwezoqoqosho kwisigaba senguqu, kwaye ibeka ilizwe elilodwa kwiithintelo ezilungelelanisiweyo kunye nohlaselo olusemthethweni oluvela kwinkqubo endala yezemali kwiinkundla zorhwebo lwamazwe ngamazwe. Umanyano olukhulu, olunqumla imida olunomthetho wezoqoqosho ofanayo luyimfuneko yemathematika. Okukhona imarike ekwabelwana ngayo inkulu, kokukhona ubuninzi bezinto ezibonakalayo obupheleleyo buphunyezwa ngokukhawuleza ngeluphu ezimeleyo (AAASR).

Injongo yokugqibela yalomanyano kukulingana okupheleleyo kweplanethi apho imida yamazwe amadala ichithwa. Oku kuqinisekisa wonke ummi ongamakhosi inkululeko epheleleyo, esemthethweni yokushiya iindawo eziyimiqolomba okanye ezimhlophe ukuze bahlale ngokukhululekileyo, baphile, kwaye bonwabele imveliso engenakukhuhlana nayo yoomatshini kwiindawo ezifanelekileyo, ezitshisayo zomhlaba.



=======================================



G.   Imbiza Enkulu kunye nokuVulwa koButyebi beSizwe



Ukuze uqonde uhlengahlengiso lwesakhiwo salo Mbhalo, umntu kufuneka aqale aqonde ubunjani bokwenyani bemali ngaphakathi kwale ndlela ikhoyo. Imali ebizwa namhlanje ngokuba "yingxowa-mali karhulumente," eneneni, ngamandla adityanisiweyo abemi. IManifesto uyayitshintsha le nkqubo ngesimemezelo esinye, esisisiseko: Uqoqosho lwesizwe kufuneka lusebenzele ikamva lesizwe, hayi amaxwebhu ebhalansi emali yamaziko eebhanki zabucala.


1.  Ukusuka kwiiNgxowa-mali ezingenzi nto ukuya kwiNguquko eSebenzayo

Siyayeka ukujonga uhlahlo-lwabiwo mali lukarhulumente njengengqokelela yamanani anomda, athintelayo. Ngokuba amazwe angamalungu oManyano anqumle onke amaqhina kunye nenkqubo yeebhanki zabucala, "imbiza enkulu" iyavulwa kuyo yonke imakethi ekwabelwana ngayo. Ezi zibonelelo zidityanisiweyo zisetyenziswa ngokukhawuleza ukufumana, ukuphuhlisa, nokusasaza inqanawa yoomatshini enqumla imida efunekayo ukwenza ubukho bungabinaxabiso ngokupheleleyo kumhlali ngamnye ngaphakathi koManyano.


2.  Ukuphela kweNgqiqo yeTyala

IManifesto awusaqapheli nayiphi na imfuno "yokuboleka" imali eyinkunzi kwiimarike zemali. Xa urhulumente ebuyisa ilungelo lakhe lokukhuphela imali exhaswa kuphela ngamandla emveliso yesizwe, isixhobo sokunyanzela kunye nesabhokhwe semarike yamatyala ziye ziphelelwe lixesha ngokupheleleyo.



3.  OkuPhambili ngokweQhinga

Ezi ngxowa-mali zivuliweyo ziya kuthi, ngaphandle kokukhetha, zixhase iintsika ezintathu ezingundoqo:


A.  Ingqalasizinda yokuSinda

Ukwenziwa ngokupheleleyo nokuzenzekela kunye nokukhuselwa kwamandla, ukuveliswa kwamanzi, kunye nothutho.


B.  Imveliso eyiRobhothi ngokupheleleyo

Ukusetyenziswa kwemveliso ezimeleyo apho oomatshini bakhulula uluntu ngokuhambisa ubunewunewu kunye nokuzimela ngokwezinto eziphathekayo njengomgangatho wentlalo ongathelelaniswayo, obonakala ngobuninzi obupheleleyo bomgangatho ophezulu kakhulu.


C.  ILogistics yeGeopolitiki

Ulawulo lwenkqubo kunye nokwenziwa kwemfuduko ukuya kwiindawo zemozulu ezisebenza ngobushushu.



=======================================



H.   ULawulo lobuChwepheshe kunye noLawulo lweMithombo



Kwesi sahluko, kusekwa izikhokelo zolawulo zendlela ihlabathi elitshintshela ngayo kuqoqosho olukhululekileyo olunoorobhothi ngokupheleleyo.


1.  ULongamo lokuLawula kuMatshini wokuPrinta iMali

IManifesto uthatha ngokukhawuleza ulawulo lwebhanki enkulu. Urhulumente unelungelo elikhethekileyo lokudala imali esemthethweni yelizwe: Imali yeLongamo. Oku kubonisa amandla okuvelisa, hayi amatyala.


2.  IMveliso yeSizwe ePheleleyo (GNR) kunye nePaki yooMatshini beSizwe

Injongo kukuba yonke imveliso ilawulwe yipaki yoomatshini yesizwe ezimeleyo elawulwa nguMgaqo-siseko wooMatshini. Oomatshini bangabanini babo ukuze kuqinisekiswe ukuba imveliso ayinakuze ibe yeyabucala okanye ihluthwe, kodwa iya kuhlala isebenzela abantu njengamandla endalo aqhelekileyo. Utyalo-mali kwi-AI lubekwe phambili ukufikelela kwinqanaba apho iindleko zemveliso ziba nguziro.


3.  Isabelo seLongamo: Indlela eya kuButyebi

Ngexesha lesigaba senguqu, wonke ummi uqinisekiswa iSabelo seLongamo sonyaka. Oku kwenza wonke ummi azimele ngokwezemali kwaye kuvala umsantsa oya kwihlabathi elitsha. Esi Sabelo sixhaswa ngemali ngokuthi urhulumente aphinde aqondise iimali zebhajethi ezikhoyo ukusuka kumthwalo wokondla inkqubo yakudala ukuya kwiintlawulo ezithe ngqo, njengoko ukwenziwa kweerobhothi kwehlisa iindleko zizonke zoluntu.



=======================================



I.   INdawo yokuVelisa yaseMntla-Atlantiki (NAPZ)



1.  Igumbi leNjini lePlanethi

IYurophu noMntla Melika ukusukela ngoku bachazwa ngumsebenzi wabo wobuchwepheshe njengendawo yokusebenzela esembindini kunye nendlu yokugcina izityalo zeplanethi.


2.  Ukuzenzekela okuPheleleyo

Ngaphakathi kwe-NAPZ, umsebenzi womntu uyayeka ukubakho. Iifama ezithe nkqo kunye namasimi enziwe ngoomatshini avelisa ukutya ubusuku nemini. Iifektri zivelisa iimveliso ezinomgangatho ongunaphakade kuba injongo yengeniso icinywe ngokupheleleyo.


3.  Amandla: ISiseko

I-NAPZ igutyungelwe luthungelwano olunxibeleleneyo lwamandla enyukliya, ifusion, kunye nemithombo evuselelekayo. Xa amandla engabizi mali, eyona ndleko inkulu yoluntu enye iyanyamalala.


4.  Ukupheliswa kweeNdawo zokuHlala ezisisigxina

I-NAPZ ngummandla ogcinelwe oomatshini. Iindawo ezibandayo nezimnyama zaseYurophu naseMntla Melika ziyapheliswa njengeendawo zokuhlala zabantu ezisisigxina. Izixeko ezingamafa ziguqulwa zibe ngamaziko ezothutho ezizisebenzelayo.


5.  Umbono weYurophu nowaseMntla Melika

Xa iYurophu noMntla Melika sele benziwe iirobhothi ngokupheleleyo kwaye bevelisa isivuno esaneleyo sabemi baseYurophu, baseMntla Melika, kwaye—kwinqanaba lokugqibela—abemi boMzantsi Melika, isigaba esilandelayo siyavulwa. Umthamo wemveliso yaseMntla Melika uyanda ukuze uxhase ngokupheleleyo ilizwekazi loMzantsi Melika, nto leyo ekhulula imveliso yaseYurophu kwaye iphinde iqondiswe kusasazo olucwangcisiweyo kwamanye amazwekazi, ngelixa kugcinwa amandla okwandisa okuqhubekekayo njengoko kufuneka.


6.  Isiseko soShishino

I-NAPZ ayichazwa yimida yesizwe yemveli, kodwa ngezibonelelo zayo ezidibeneyo, ezizisebenzelayo. Injongo yayo yodwa kukwenziwa ngobuninzi kwenqanawa yehlabathi jikelele yoomatshini, ukuqhubela phambili kobukrelekrele bokwenziwa obucwangcisiweyo, kunye nokuveliswa kweeplani zobuchwepheshe eziphambili ezifunekayo ukufezekisa ubuninzi obugqibeleleyo beplanethi.


7.  Ukudityaniswa kweLogistics

Lo mmandla usebenza ngokwentsebenziswano ethe ngqo kunye neendawo zokuhlala ezitshisayo kunye namacandelo ezibonelelo ezisemazantsi. Ngelixa iindawo ezitshisayo zibonelela ngeemeko ezifanelekileyo zobushushu zokuhlala kwabantu kunye nokuchacha ngolongamo, i-NAPZ isebenza njengendawo yokubumbela yoshishino olunzima—ilungisa imathiriyeli ekrwada kwaye isebenzise uthungelwano lwezothutho oluzimeleyo ukusasaza izinto ezigqityiweyo kunye noomatshini simahla ngokupheleleyo kulo lonke uManyano lweMarike yeLongamo.


8.  Ukuhlelwa ngokweSakhiwo

Njengoko imveliso yefektri ezenzekelayo ngaphakathi kwe-NAPZ ifikelela kumthamo omkhulu, igalelo layo elikhulayo kwiMveliso yeSizwe ePheleleyo (GNR) likhawulezisa ukuphelelwa lixesha okuqinisekileyo kweemali eziyimfezeko ezingamafa, okuvula indlela yokuphunyezwa ngokupheleleyo koMgaqo-siseko wooMatshini.

J.   Ikhaya Elitsha



Ukufuduselwa kwiindawo ezitshisayo akubonisi nje ukutshintsha kwendawo, kodwa kukuhlaziywa ngokupheleleyo kwendalo yokuphila yomntu. Sishiya ngemva izakhiwo zekhonkrithi kunye neendleko zokugcina ezingapheliyo zeendawo ezinemozulu ebandayo.


1.  INdawo yokuHlala eTshisayo

NgoMbhalo, imfuduko enkulu yobushushu yenziwa ukuya kwiindawo apho uluntu luyilelwe ngokwebhayoloji ukuba luhlale khona.


2.  Iindawo eziPhakamileyo eziTshisayo: Indawo eKhuselekileyo

Kwibloko yaseNtshona, ukhetho lomntla woMzantsi Melika lusekelwe ekuzinzeni kwetectonics kunye nokusebenza ngokufanelekileyo kobushushu. Apha, imfuno yeenkqubo zokufudumeza icinywe ngokupheleleyo.


3.  UYilo loBuninzi

Izindlu ezizii-modular ezinomgangatho ongunaphakade zihambiswa zivela kwi-NAPZ. Ngaphakathi kwale mimandla, akukho rhwebo lukhoyo; yonke into isebenza njengemisebenzi edityanisiweyo ngaphakathi kweziseko zophuhliso.


4.  Ubuchwepheshe ngeNdawo

Oku kukutshintshiselana ngokulinganayo. Iimveliso zecandelo loshishino zibonelelwa zivela kumda weYurophu, ngelixa uMzantsi Melika ubonelela ngommandla. Xa la malungu mabini edibana, kuvela uqoqosho olukhululekileyo oluthe ngqo kwilizwekazi loMzantsi Melika kubo bonke abemi bangoku nabekamva. Akukho mali ijikelezayo—siyayicima imfuno yayo.

IManifesto awuchazi iprojekthi yokuthumela kwelinye ilizwe yaseYurophu, kodwa umanyano lwehlabathi jikelele. Izizwe ezikwimimandla etshisayo aziyo "zindlu zokubamba iindwendwe"; zingamahlakani angabaseki angena ngqo kuqoqosho olukhululekileyo kwaye angabanini beziseko zophuhliso ngokwemiqathango elinganayo namazwe asemantla.


5.  Ilungelo leLongamo leZindlu

Ngaphakathi komanyano lweerobhothi, indlu ayisiyiyo into yorhwebo okanye umthombo wetyala lobomi bonke. Wonke ummi uqinisekiswa ikhaya lobuchwepheshe obuphezulu, elakhiwe kwaye ligcinwe yipaki yoomatshini. Azikho iirenti, akukho zikweledu zezindlu, kwaye akukho rhafu yepropati. Lilungelo elisisiseko ngokupheleleyo.


6.  Ukugqwesa kobuChwepheshe

Amakhaya akhiwa kusetyenziswa izinto ezinomgangatho ongunaphakade, akhululwe ngokupheleleyo kwiimfuno zokulungiswa kwekamva. Yonke into ukusuka kulawulo lwemozulu kunye nokucocwa kwamanzi ukuya ekucocweni okuzenzekelayo kunye nolawulo lwenkunkuma kudityaniswe kwisakhiwo sesakhiwo kwaye ilawulwa ngokuzimeleyo.


7.  UYilo oluvisisanayo

Ekubeni yonke into yakhiwa kwimimandla enomlinganiselo wonyaka ophakathi ozinzileyo nomnandi, uyilo lunokuvulwa. Imida phakathi kwezithuba ezingaphakathi nangaphandle iyachithwa. Izakhiwo zidityaniswe kwindalo etshisayo, zineenkqubo zamandla elanga ezizigcinayo kunye neegadi ezithe nkqo.

3.  IiReferendamu zeQhinga kunye noNyulo oluNgemanga

L.   URulumente njengeShishini eliHlangeneyo



1.  URulumente: Ishishini laKho eliValelweyo

Kule nkqubo yanamhlanje, ungunmnini wesizwe, kodwa awuvumelekanga ukuba usisebenzise. Cinga ngoRulumente njengeshishini elikhulu apho wena kunye nabo bonke abanye abemi ningabanini zabelo. Kodwa eli shishini—amandla ethu ahlanganisiweyo—lihleli entolongweni, libotshwe ngamatyathanga emva kweentsimbi ngebhola netyathanga "letyala" kwaye lithatyathelw' indawo sisilo sezoqoqosho, xa yonke into uRulumente awayeyifuna yayikukhululeka kwakhe. Ngelixa iinkampani zabucala zibaleka zijikeleza ngokuhamba ngokupheleleyo kwaye zivelisa umgangatho ophantsi ukwandisa ingeniso, isixhobo sabemi ngokwabo (uRulumente) sihleli sikhubazekile kwaye singenakho ukusebenza. Sixelelwe ukuba uRulumente "akanakukwazi ukuyihlawulela," ukuba akanazo iingalo nemilenze yokwakha ikamva. Inyaniso kukuba uRulumente uhleli emva kweentsimbi kuba iibhanki zabucala kunye nenkqubo endala bathathe isitshixo somatshini wokushicilela imali.


Kodwa IManifesto sisitshixo esivula isisele.


2.  SiyaMbuyisela URulumente Imilenze YaKhe

Ngokuvula imveliso eyirobhothi (Per Machina), uRulumente akasadingi "kucela imvume" okanye ukurhafisa abemi bakhe ukuze enze izinto. Oomatshini ziingalo nemilenze emitsha kaRulumente.


3.  SiyiTyumza Ibhola neTyathanga

Ngokubuyisa ilungelo lokudala imali yolongamo, ityala ebeligcine uRulumente esihlalweni esinamavili liyacinywa.


4.  Ukusuka eBanjweni ukuya kuMyili weZakhiwo

Xa uRulumente ephakama kwisisele sakhe sasentolongweni, uyayeka ukuba bubukhulumentu obunzima. Utshintsha abe ngumatshini wezothutho osebenza kakuhle ohambisa iSabelo saKho seLongamo kwaye wakhe iparadesi kwiindawo ezitshisayo.

M.   IBhanki yaBucala iya kuCima iPesenti eFana neyokuNyuka kwe-GNR



Iibhanki zabucala ziyanyanzelwa ukuba zicime amatyala abemi ngesantya esifana ncam nesiye inyuke ngaso iMveliso yeSizwe ePheleleyo (GNR). Njengoko uqoqosho olukhululekileyo lukhula, inzuzo yemali yeenkampani iyancipha, ngelixa izinto zabo zexabiso zokwenyani zisonyuka simahla. Iibhanki azinakuvunyelwa ke ngoko ukuba zifune inzala epheleleyo, njengoko iindleko zokuphila kwabanini beebhanki nazo zehla ziye kuziro.

Lo ngumongo wesakhiwo semali sohlengahlengiso. Iibhanki zabucala kufuneka "zipheliswe" njengoko iMveliso yeSizwe ePheleleyo (GNR) yoqoqosho olukhululekileyo ikhula. Oku kwenza inguqu egudileyo apho ixabiso lemali lisuka ematyaleni liya kumthamo wemveliso.



Nasi esona sisombululo sisebenzayo:


1.  "Isalathisi sokuPhelisa" (iTyala-ngokuchasene-naSimahla)

Yazisa indlela esemthethweni ebizwa ngokuba kukuCinywa kweTyala okuZenzekelayo.


A.  Ingqiqo

Urhulumente ulinganisa ukuba yeyiphi inxalenye yeemfuno ezisisiseko zabemi (ukutya, amandla, izindlu, ezothutho) ngoku ebonelelwa simahla ngeepaki zerobhothi zesizwe.


B.  Indlela yokuSebenza

Ukuba i-20% yeemfuno zobomi iye yaba simahla, iibhanki zabucala ziyanyanzelwa ngumthetho ukuba zicime i-20% yawo onke amatyala abucala. Inzala iyamiswa okanye incitshiswe ngesantya esihambelanayo.


C.  Ingxoxo

Abasemabhankini abanakukhalaza ngezizathu zokuziphatha, kuba neendleko zabo zokuphila zikwazehla nge-20%. Ababi ngamahlwempu ngokomgangatho wobomi, kuphela ngokwamanani akwisikrini.



2.  Ukuphathwa "kweeBhanki eziNobutshaba" kuMazwe aNgaphandle

Oku yeyona nto iyinkxalabo enkulu—ukuba iibhanki ziya kubaleka kwaye zisebenzise amanye amazwe njengesiseko sokuhlasela umanyano lohlengahlengiso.



Isisombululo kukuValelwa kweZimali:


A.  Amandla oManyano

Nje ukuba umanyano lohlengahlengiso (amazwe athe avula umbhalo) lusekiwe, luya kwazisa uthintelo olupheleleyo ngokuchasene neebhanki ezisebenza kumazwe anobutshaba.


B.  Ukutshintsha kweXabiso

Ekubeni umanyano lohlengahlengiso lulawula imveliso yokwenyani (iirobhothi kunye nemathiriyeli ekrwada), imali yeebhanki zangaphandle ngokukhawuleza iba zizicwili zephepha ezingenaxabiso. Abanako "ukuthenga" nantoni na kumanyano lohlengahlengiso ngemali yabo yamatyala amadala.


C.  Ukhetho lweeBhanki zaBucala

Nokuba zihlale zibe yinxalenye yeqela elitsha lolawulo lwenkonzo yoluntu kuqoqosho olukhululekileyo oluya ekwenziweni iirobhothi ngokupheleleyo, okanye zibalekele kumazwe aza kuwa ngokukhawuleza ngenxa yokuba loo mazwe akanakho ukufikelela kumanyano lohlengahlengiso kunye nemveliso yalo yerobhothi.



3.  Indima yeBhanki eNkulu: Ukusuka kwiNzala ukuya kuMlinganiselo weziBonelelo

IBhanki eNkulu iyayeka ukujonga ukunyuka kwamaxabiso kunye namazinga enzala. Endaweni yoko, iba liOfisi yeziBonelelo.


A.  Ukusuka kwiBhanki eNkulu ukuya kwiOfisi yeziBonelelo

IOfisi yeziBonelelo endaweni yoko ilinganisa i-GNR, indlalifa ye-GDP eyenziwe yesizwe. Xa i-GNR igubungela i-100 ekhulwini leemfuno zabemi, uLongamo oluPheleleyo luyabhengezwa. Ngelo xesha, yonke imali namatyala ayekeleli ukubakho phantsi komthetho womgaqo-siseko.


B.  Ukucaca

Ngokuba nale le icacileyo (umz., i-10%, i-50%, i-100%), iinkokeli zezizwe ezizayo kumanyano lohlengahlengiso zibona ngqo ukuba indlela injani. Iba yinkqubo enokuqikelelwa endaweni yesiphithiphithi.


C.  Amandla eeBhanki aLungelelananga

IManifesto wenza ukuchithwa okulawulwayo kwenkqubo yamatyala. Njengoko oomatshini bekhulula uluntu kwiindleko, uluntu nalo luyakhululwa ematyaleni alo. Asilwi neebhanki ngezixhobo, kodwa ngezishwankathelo zemathematika ezenza amandla azo angafuneki.

Ingxelo engentla isebenza njengenkcazelo yobuchwepheshe "yoHlaziyo olungabonakaliyo" ekusebenzeni.

Isisombululo kukuba kube nomthetho, il e ehambelanayo apho ukukhululwa ngezemali kwabemi kwenzeka ngesantya esifana ncam nesokwanda kwemveliso yokwenyani—iMveliso yeSizwe ePheleleyo (GNR).

4.  Uphononongo lweZigaba zeLe yokuKhululeka

IsiGaba 1: 25% GNR – Ukudutyulwa kokuQala kokuQala


Esi sesona sigaba sibalulekileyo sohlengahlengiso, apho umqolo wengqondo nowezoqoqosho wenkqubo endala waphulwa. Xa iirobhothi ezizingabanini bazo zomanyano zenze ngokuzenzekela imveliso kunye nokusasazwa kokutya okusisiseko kunye nonikezelo lwamandla elizwe (umbane kunye nokufudumeza), i-GNR ye-25% iyabhengezwa.

Ngaxeshanye, isishwankathelo sokuqala esisemthethweni ngokuchasene neebhanki zabucala siyaphunyezwa: i-25% yetyala lommi ngamnye liphela (iimali mboleko zezindlu, iimali mboleko zabathengi, iimali mboleko zabafundi) iyacinywa kwiiseva zeebhanki. Ngenxa yokuba umbane nokutya ngoku kusimahla, iindleko zokuphila kwabanikazi beebhanki ziyehla, nto leyo eyenza amabango abo enzala angavumeleki ngokwemathematika. Ukuqinisekisa ukuba abemi banokuthengisa kumacandelo angekenziwa ngoomatshini, inqanaba lokuqala leSabelo seLongamo liyavulwa—intlawulo ethe ngqo yemali yolongamo evela kwiBhanki eNkulu.

IsiGaba 2: 50% GNR – Ukuphula uBukhethekileyo beZindlu


Kwisigaba esilandelayo, ukwenziwa kwerobhothi kuyanda ukuya kwicandelo lezokwakha, ulondolozo, kunye nezothutho zoluntu kunye nempahla. Xa izindlu kunye nezothutho sele zifakwe kwinkqubo yasimahla yomanyano, ilizwe lifikelela kwi-50% ye-GNR.

Kwesi sigaba, isiqingatha sawo onke amatyala abemi nawombuso ayacinywa kwiincwadi zeebhanki. Imarike yezindlu, eyathi ngexesha leminyaka yamatyala yasebenza njengesona sixhobo siphambili sokugcina abemi bevalelwe kwimali mboleko yobomi bonke, iyawa kwimo yayo endala kwaye iphinde ivele njengelungelo lomntu. Ngaxeshanye, iSabelo seLongamo silungiswa phezulu ngokunxulumene nokwanda kwemveliso ye-GNR (indlalifa ye-GDP eyenziwe yesizwe) yipaki yoomatshini.

IsiGaba 3: 75% GNR – Ukudiliza isiCupho soBunewunewu


Xa i-75% yeemfuno zizonke zezibonelelo zoluntu zigutyungelwe ngokupheleleyo ngaphandle komsebenzi womntu—kuquka ubuchwepheshe obuphambili, imfundo, ukhathalelo lwezempilo, kunye nezinto zomgangatho ophezulu ezichazwe kwisahluko esingengoMgaqo-siseko weNtlanganisela—i-75% yawo onke amatyala aseleyo iyawa.

Kweli nqanaba, amashishini abucala kunye neebhanki ngokwenyani baphulukene namandla abo okunyanzela abantu ukuba basebenze. Uninzi lwabemi lukhetha ukuphila ngokupheleleyo ngonikezelo lwasimahla lomanyano kunye neSabelo sabo seLongamo, nto leyo enyanzela iinkampani zabucala zokugqibela ukuba zitshone okanye zinikezele ngemigca yazo yemveliso kwizakhiwo ezizimeleyo.

O.   "Isicupho soBunewunewu" kunye noMsebenzi oNyanzelekileyo oFihliweyo



"IsiCupho soBunewunewu" yindlela egcina inkqubo yamatyala amadala ixesha elide emva kokuba ilahlekelwe yimfezeko yayo yobuchwepheshe. Sisitshixo sengqondo nezoqoqosho esithintela abemi ekuboneni ukuba umsebenzi wabo awusafuneki.


1.  Inkohliso yokhetho

Phantsi kwenkqubo endala, abemi bahlala bekholelwa ukuba umsebenzi wabo uyimfuneko ukuze uluntu lusebenze. Ngokujinga isithembiso "sobuwewunewu" kunye "nesimo" (esibizwa ngokuba siCupho soBunewunewu), inkqubo iyabatsalela abantu ematyaleni ngokuzithandela. Logama nje abemi bekholelwa ukuba kufuneka basebenze ukuze "bakwazi ukuhlawula" indlela yabo yokuphila, bahlala bevalelwe kumjikelo wetyala. Abaqapheli ukuba izinto abasokolisayo ukuzifumana bezingaveliswa simahla yipaki yerobhothi, ukuba nje isakhiwo sobunini besitshintshiwe.


2.  Umsebenzi oNyanzelekileyo oFihliweyo

Umsebenzi owenziwa ngabemi namhlanje, ubukhulu becala, "ngumsebenzi onyanzelekileyo ofihliweyo." Awuyomfuneko kwimveliso yeemfuno zoluntu zokusinda, kodwa kulondolozo lwenkqubo yemali. Sivelisa izinto ezingadingi ukuveliswa, kwizakhiwo ezikhoyo kuphela ukuvelisa inzala yeebhanki. Xa ubuchwepheshe bokwenziwa kwerobhothi ngokupheleleyo bukhona, iyure nganye yomsebenzi womntu echithwa kwimisebenzi etyumza umphefumlo okanye engenamsebenzi bubusela bexesha lomntu—ubusela obusebenza kuphela ukugcina ilungelo labanini-matyala.


3.  Ukwaphula isiTshixo

Umanyano lolungiso luyasicima isiCupho soBunewunewu ngokuthi endaweni yezinto "zobunewunewu" kufakwe ubuninzi obusimahla, obusemgangathweni ophezulu, obuveliswe ngoomatshini. Xa ummi ebona ukuba izinto ebefudula encama inkululeko yakhe ukuze "akwazi ukuzihlawula" ngoku zifumaneka kuye wonke umntu, simahla ngokupheleleyo, isitshixo sengqondo senkqubo yamatyala siyaphulwa.

    • ISicupho soBunewunewu sisithintelo sokugqibela senkqubo endala.

    • Nje ukuba abemi baqonde ukuba "IsiCupho soBunewunewu" lukhetho, ayisiyomfuneko, inguqu eya kulongamo olupheleleyo iba yinto engenakuphetshwa.

Injongo yesi sahluko kukukhanyisela indlela, ukuze umntu ngamnye akwazi ukuphuma kwisicupho aze angene enkululekweni eyenziwa kukwenziwa kwerobhothi.

P.   UMgaqo-siseko weNtlanganisela



Enye yezona mpazamo zinkulu zeqhinga umanyano lolungiso lwekamva olunokuyenza kukukholelwa ukuba umsebenzi ugqityiwe xa iimfuno ezisisiseko zabemi (ukutya, umbane, izindlu, kunye nezothutho) sele zikhuselwe simahla. Ukuyeka apho kukungena kwisicupho esibulalayo.

Ukuba urhulumente wenza kuphela imveliso esisiseko kodwa ushiye ummandla wepremiyam nobunewunewu (ubugcisa, uyilo oluphambili, ifenitshala yomgangatho ophezulu, izithuthi ezikhethekileyo, kunye namava akhethekileyo) ungakhathalelwanga, inkqubo yetyala labucala iya kuphinda ivele ngenkangeleko entsha, efihliweyo.

Oosomashishini abanobuqhetseba kunye nababesakuba ngabasemabhankini baya kwakha ngokukhawuleza iifemu ezikhethekileyo. Baya kuphendukela ebantwini bathi: "Kulungile, urhulumente ukunika ukutya okusemgangathweni simahla kunye nekhaya elisemgangathweni simahla, kodwa uyayifuna le mveliso yodwa, ethungwe ngesandla? Uyawufuna lo mzobo uprintiweyo omhle okanye le nto yobunewunewu? Ke ngoko kufuneka uze kwaye usebenze nzima kwishishini lam iinyanga ezintathu kuqala."

Ngequbuliso, kuvele uqoqosho olungqameneyo, apho abantu baphinda banyanzelwe ukuba bathengise ixesha labo eliyigugu nenkululeko ukuze baphumeze umgangatho wobomi kunye nemo. Ubukhoboka bezoqoqosho abukapheliswa; buvele banyakazele phantsi kumgangatho omnye ukuya kwicandelo lobunewunewu.


UMgaqo-siseko weNtlanganisela

Ukuze oku kuthintelwe, IManifesto kufuneka usebenzise uMgaqo-siseko weNtlanganisela, uguqule ngokomgaqo-siseko uluntu lube kukwenziwa ngokupheleleyo nokungathelelanisiyo kweerobhothi kuzo zonke iindawo zemveliso. Ummisela ukuba ukwenziwa kwerobhothi ngokungaphelelanga kukukhohlisa. Inkululeko iphelele—umntu akanakuba nesaqingatha ekhululekile. Injongo ekwabelwana ngayo ngurhulumente nabemi bawo ayinakuze ibe kukugcina abantu bephila nje; injongo, ngomanyano, kukubonelela wonke umntu ngobuninzi obupheleleyo bowona mgangatho uphakamileyo onokucingwa.

Ngokwesi sibophelelo ekwabelwana ngaso, uluntu lubotshelelwe ngokomgaqo-siseko ekwenziweni ngokupheleleyo nokungathelelanisiyo kweerobhothi kuzo zonke iindawo zemveliso. Ipaki yoomatshini kunye neenkqubo zayo ze-AI ziba yinto ephilayo, eyabelwe wonke umntu ehlala ivela kwaye ingazange ivunyelwe ukuba igcwale ibe ngumgangatho ongwevu, omncinci.


IsiQinisekiso soMgangatho kunye neZinto eziHlala ngoNaphakade

Impendulo karhulumente kuxhaphazo lwabucala kunye neenzame zokumisela ngokutsha ubukhoboka akufuneki ibe kukuthintela okanye ubuzwilakhe. Impendulo karhulumente kukhuphiswano ngobuninzi.

Ukuba ifemu yabucala izama ukwenza abantu basebenze ukuze bafumane into entle okanye imveliso yobugcisa, kungumsebenzi ocacileyo womanyano ukucwangcisa nokuqondisa ipaki yerobhothi ukuba ivelise into efana ncam naleyo—okanye ebhetele.


Urhulumente uya kuvelisa:


•  Ukugqibelela kwejiyometri:

Iimveliso ezigqitha kubuchule bezandla zomntu.


•  Izinto ezihlala ngonaphakade:

Izinto ezakhelwe ukuhlala kwizizukulwana, ngaphandle kokuphelelwa lixesha okufakwe ngaphakathi.


•  Ukugqwesa kobugcisa:

Iirobhothi ezifakelwe i-AI ezikwaziyo ukuprinta eyona mizobo mihle, ukubumba eyona mifanekiso iqingqiweyo mihle, nokwakha ezona zinto zobunewunewu ziphambili, kwaye zenze zifumaneke kubo bonke abemi simahla ngokupheleleyo.


Xa oomatshini bakarhulumente begqitha kwicandelo labucala kuzo zonke iindawo—ukusuka kweyona ndidi incinci yengqolowa ukuya kweyona nto yobunewunewu intsonkothileyo—inkuthazo yokuba abantu bazithobe kwinjongo yengeniso yomnye umntu iyanyamalala. Kuphela xa ubuninzi buphelele, kwaye umgangatho uphelele, usizi lwenkqubo yamatyala endala lungcwatywa ngonaphakade.

Q.   Ukucocwa okuKhulu – Ukusuka kwiLifa lokuTshona ukuya kuLongamo



1.  URulumente njengoMphathi wokuTshona okungapheliyo

2.  Inkululeko yokuZiphatha

Ekubeni urhulumente okwimo yakhe yangoku elilifa lokutshona eliphantsi kohlutho, thina njengabemi asinawo umthwalo wokuziphatha wokuhlawula "amatyala" akhe angengowamvelo. Ilifa lokutshona alinakufuna intembeko kwabo libabulawé yindlala. Oku kusinika ilungelo elipheleleyo lokuphuma nje kwisisele samatyala kwaye siyivule iMgaqo-siseko wooMatshini. Asisengabo abanamatyala—siziindlalifa zobuninzi bobuchwepheshe ekumele ngoku busasazwe.



3.  Isixhobo sokuNyanzela esiNgqiqweni: UMatshini ngokuchasene noMntu

Ukuze uqonde ukuba kutheni inkqubo yangoku ililifa lokutshona, umntu kufuneka ajonge inyaniso yobuchwepheshe. Umntu, xa ethelekiswa nerobhothi, uyiyunithi yemveliso ebiza kakhulu kwaye eethe-ethe:


A.  Ukufumaneka

Umntu usebenza malunga ne-22% yeeyure zonyaka (emva kokulala, ixesha lokuphumla, kunye neholide). Irobhothi lisebenza i-100%.


B.  Umda wempazamo

Irobhothi elinolusu lwenzwa kunye nombono weenzwa we-360-digri liyayisusa into yomntu kwaye linciphise umda wempazamo ube lixabiso elinguziro lobalo ngexesha lemisebenzi ephindaphindwayo.


C.  Ukutsalwa kweNtlalo

Umntu ubiza i-100% kumvuzo + malunga ne-30–50% kwimirhumo yokhuselo lwentlalo, i-inshorensi, kunye nolawulo. Irobhothi libiza i-0% kwimirhumo yokhuselo lwentlalo. Alifuni mhlalaphantsi, akukho khefu lokugula, kwaye akukho gumbi lokuphumla.


D.  Isiphetho

Ukusebenzisa umsebenzi womntu kwimveliso enokwenziwa ngoomatshini kunesohlwayo sendleko esingaphezulu kwe-1 000%. Yile "ntlawulo yesohlwayo somntu" amaqela ohlutho anyanzela abantu ukuba bayihlawule ngeerhafu namaxabiso aphezulu.



4.  Ukwenzakala okuNgokuchaseneyo noMsebenzi


A.  Uncedo lweRobhothi

Umatshini oxhotyiswe ngezinzwa ezibambekayo kunye nokuskena kwerada okuqhubekekayo akunakuze kufuneke "utyumkile wamnyama nokuhlaza." Uva indawo yakho engqongileyo nge-360 digri kwaye ushukuma ngokuchaneka kwejiyometri umntu, ngokwebhayoloji, angenakuze ayifumane.


B.  Ukuziphatha

Ukunyanzela umntu ukuba enze umsebenzi owenzwa ngcono ngumatshini olawulwa zizinzwa akusiyonto nje yokungasebenzi kakuhle—yinkohlakalo.

Oku kulandelayo ngokwenjenje kumiselwe kumgaqo-siseko wesizwe:

Njengoko ubukho bubonelelwa nge-Per Machina, ilungelo likaRulumente lokurhafisa ixesha okanye ingeniso yomntu liyaphela. Ummi ungamakhosi kwaye ukhululwe ebukhobokeni bezoqoqosho.

Inkcazelo: Esi sihlomelo simisela isithintelo solwimi nesomthetho kuluntu olutsha. Ngokuchaza i-Per Machina ngqo phakathi komgaqo-siseko, onke amalinge okukhohlisa ngezivumelwano ayamiswa. Ukuba iziko labucala okanye likarhulumente lisebenzisa igama elithi "Per Machina" kwisivumelwano, isiqinisekiso, okanye inkcazelo yenkonzo, kuba yinto engenakwenzeka ngokomgaqo-siseko ukubandakanya imali, iintlawulo ezifihliweyo, okanye izisombululo zamatyala (njengentlawulo ebambezelekileyo). Naliphi na ilinge lokufihla iimfuno zemali emva kweli gama lenza isivumelwano singabikho, kwaye kufuneka sithathwe njengolwaphulo-mgaqo-siseko.

    • Ingqiqo kukuba umsebenzi woomatshini wahluliwe, inkqubo yerhafu icinyiwe, kwaye zonke izikroba zemali zivalwe ngokupheleleyo.


Ubume obuMthethweni beSabelo seLongamo

§3.  Ubunjani beSabelo seLongamo

ISabelo seLongamo senza ilungelo lommi elikhuselwe ngumgaqo-siseko, elingokwemvelo kubuninzi obubonakalayo obuveliswe ngoomatshini abazisebenzelayo, abangabakarhulumente. Ngokobunjani baso bunikezelwa ngurhulumente ngokukhethekileyo kwaye akukho phantsi kwaziphi iimeko apho bunokukhutshwa, bulawulwe, okanye buchukunyiswe ziinkampani zabucala, iibhanki zorhwebo, okanye amaqumrhu angaphandle aphethe amazwe. Inkcazelo: Lo mhlathi ufaka ibhulorho eqinisekileyo yezoqoqosho ngqo kumgaqo-siseko. Ngokumisela ubume obusekelwe ematshinini ngokukhethekileyo beSabelo seLongamo, inkqubo yemali yabucala iyathintelwa kuwo nawaphi na amalinge okuyilinganisa nezabelo, iimali mboleko zokuqikelela, okanye imali eyimali yesiqhelo. Ngexesha lesigaba senguqu—xa uluntu lwangaphakathi (olufana nokutya, iimoto, nombane) lubonelelwa simahla ngokupheleleyo nge-Per Machina, kodwa ihlabathi langaphandle lisalawulwa yinkqubo endala yamatyala kunye nemali—iSabelo seLongamo siqinisekisa ukuba intsalela enkulu, eveliswe ngoomatshini abazisebenzelayo, yokuthumela kwelinye ilizwe karhulumente isasazwa ngqo ebantwini ngendlela yeendlela zokuhlawula zokwenyani, zangaphandle. Oku kunika abantu amandla ezoqoqosho okugcina inkululeko yabo yezenzo kumazwe ngamazwe, ukubaleka ubusika baseMntla njengabakhenkethi bolongamo, okanye ukufumana umhlaba kwiindawo eziphakamileyo ezitshisayo, kude kube lusuku apho ihlabathi lonke liye lamkela umgaqo-siseko woomatshini kwaye nemali ipheliswe ngokupheleleyo kuyo yonke indawo.

    • Ngokwengqiqo, umsebenzi womatshini wahluliwe, inkqubo yerhafu icinyiwe, ulongamo luqinisekisiwe ngokomgaqo-siseko, kwaye zonke izikroba zemali zivaliwe ngokupheleleyo.



ISENZO SESIBINI: INGUQU YEZOQOQOSHO



§4.  Uhlengahlengiso lweBhanki kunye nokuPheliswa kweTyala okuLawulwayo (Isikali se-GNR)

Umhlathana 1. URulumente uthabatha, ngesiphumo esikhawulezileyo, ilungelo elikhethekileyo lokudala nokusasaza imali esemthethweni yesizwe ngeBhanki eNkulu. Iibhanki zorhwebo zabucala ngokwenjenje azivumelekanga ukuba zenze imali entsha ngokubolekisa.

Umhlathana 2. Njengoko umthamo wemveliso wenqanawa yerobhothi yesizwe nezizingabanini bazo (GNR) ukhula, onke amatyala abucala, iimali mboleko zezindlu, kunye namanqanaba enzala abemi phakathi kwenkqubo yeebhanki zabucala aya kucinywa ngepesenti ethe ngqo ehambelanayo. URulumente uyawamisa kwaye atsixe onke amabango enzala akhoyo kunye nezicwangciso zokunciphisa isikweledu ngexesha elichongiweyo lembali.

Umhlathana 3. Iibhanki zabucala ngokwenjenje ziyahluthwa lonke ilungelo elisemthethweni lokunyusa iimfuno zokunciphisa isikweledu, ukuxoxisana ngokutsha ngemiqathango, okanye ukufuna intlawulo ekhawulezileyo ukuvala ingeniso yenzala elahlekileyo. Wonke umthwalo wetyala nenzala uya kuncipha ngokulinganayo ngokomlinganiselo othe ngqo kubuninzi obukhulayo obuzenzekelayo.

Inkcazelo: Esi sishwankathelo sakhiwe ukuthintela ukothuka kwezoqoqosho ngexesha lesigaba senguqu. Ngokunxulumanisa ukuncitshiswa kwetyala, inzala, kunye nokuncitshiswa kwesikweledu ngqo kwisikali se-GNR, inkqubo yeebhanki zabucala iyanyamalala ngesantya esifana ncam neso loqoqosho olukhululekileyo lusasazwa ngaso. Isithintelo ngokuchasene neemfuno ezinyusiweyo zokuncitshiswa kwesikweledu sibalulekile: sinqanda iibhanki ekuzameni ngokuphithizela ukukhupha imali kubemi njengoko iingeniso zabo zenzala zisihla. Abasebenzi basebhankini kunye nabemi ke ngoko abakhe babekho phantsi kokubandezeleka kwezoqoqosho okanye ukungaphangeli; endaweni yoko, batshintshwa ngokukhuselekileyo nangokuthe chu besuka ebukhobokeni bemivuzo ukuya kulongamo oluzenzekelayo olupheleleyo.


§5.  UMgaqo-siseko wooMatshini kunye neZiseko zoPhuhliso eziZingabanini

Umhlathana 1. Wonke umthamo wemveliso omkhulu, obanzi, nozenzekelayo, kunye neziseko zophuhliso eziqhutywa yi-AI (AAASR), ngokwenjenje ziyahluthwa ubunini babucala nobukaRulumente kwaye zibhengezwe njengezinto ezizingabanini bazo.

Umhlathana 2. Oku kuphela komsebenzi osemthethweni wenqanawa yoomatshini kukuvelisa ubuninzi obubonakalayo eluntwini. Ngokwenjenje iphantsi kobutyala obuqatha bolwaphulo-mthetho ukurhafisa, ukusebenzisa amandla, okanye ukwenza inzuzo kwimveliso yasimahla eveliswe ngokuzenzekelayo. Esi sishwankathelo sibandakanya ngokukhethekileyo izixhobo zemveliso ezihlanganyelweyo nezigcina uluntu; ipropati yabucala, izixhobo zomntu siqu, kunye nezixhobo zasemakhaya zabemi zihlala zingangeneleleki.

Inkcazelo: Nguwo lo uMgaqo-siseko wooMatshini okhusela ubuninzi. Ngokubhengeza oomatshini "njengezizingabanini bazo," abatyali-zimali abazizityebi ababucala okanye amagosa karhulumente agxuphulekayo abanakuluhlutha umveliso okanye bafune inzuzo. Ubutyala obuqatha bolwaphulo-mthetho obumelene nokurhafisa buqinisekisa ukuba akukho mntu unokuzifaka phakathi komatshini nommi. Kwangelo xesha, uMhlathana 2 uvala ngempumelelo ucango kubagxeki ngokubhengeza ngokucacileyo ukuba amakhaya abantu abucala nezinto zabo (njengomatshini wokwenza isonka ekhitshini) zishiywe zizodwa ngokupheleleyo.

Ingcaciso eBalulekileyo ngokuMalunga noBumbano lweTrasti yoMthetho

URulumente, kwaye ngaloo ndlela abemi, bangabaphathiswa kodwa ayingobanini bePaki yooMatshini. IPaki yooMatshini ingabanini bePaki yooMatshini. URulumente kunye nabemi abanalo ilungelo elisemthethweni lokuthengisa iPaki yooMatshini, njengoko bengeyiyo abanini bayo. Yonke into eyongeziweyo kwiPaki yooMatshini ngokwemathiriyeli ekrwada, amandla, kunye nolwando ihlala apho ngokusisigxina. IPaki yooMatshini kufuneka ithathwe njengenxalenye ehlanganisiweyo yenkqubo yendalo kunye nemithombo yendalo yoMhlaba. Yile meko ikhethekileyo nengenakungenelelwa esekiweyo kuMgaqo-siseko wooMatshini.


§6.  ULongamo lwengqondo kaRulumente Ngexesha lesiGaba seNguqu

Ekubeni ukuzimela okupheleleyo kwezobuchwepheshe kungekaphunyezwa, kwaye ipaki yoomatshini isaphantsi kolwakhiwo, ulawulo olupheleleyo lukaRulumente phezu kobukrelekrele obuya kulawula ikamva ngokwenjenje luyasekwa. Lo mthetho awunakuguqulwa kwisigaba sonke senguqu.

Umhlathana 1. Ubunini bukaRulumente obuKhethekileyo. Bonke ubukrelekrele bokwenziwa obulawula, okanye obenzelwe ukulawula, umthamo wemveliso yesizwe, iziseko zophuhliso, kunye nokuhamba kwezibonelelo, buya kuthi bupheleleyo buphuhliswe, bubengobukaRulumente, kwaye bulawulwe nguRulumente. Akukho nxalenye yesiseko sekhowudi, iiparamitha, okanye ihardware enokugqithiselwa kumaqela angaphandle.

Umhlathana 2. UKhuselo ngokuchasene nokuSasazeka koRhwebo. Iinkampani zabucala kunye nabadlali bezorhwebo abavumelekanga ngokupheleleyo ekuphuhliseni, ekunikeni iilayisensi, okanye ekuhlanganiseni iinkqubo ze-AI eziphantsi kobunini kwingqalasizinda eqhelekileyo kaRulumente. URulumente akayi kuthenga okanye alungelelanise izisombululo zorhwebo ezingaphelelanga apho i-algorithms efihliweyo inobungozi bokonakalisa ingqiqo yenkqubo ngenjongo yenzuzo yabucala. Ingqalasizinda yobukrelekrele iya kuhlala imsulwa, iselubala, kwaye ikude kuRulumente.

Umhlathana 3. Izabelo zikaRulumente. URulumente ngezabelo ezikhethekileyo uya kukhusela izibonelelo ezingenamda zophuhliso lwenkqubo ye-AI, umthamo wokubala, kunye nokusebenza ngokufanelekileyo. Oku akusiyo utyalo-mali lorhwebo olufuna imbuyekezo yezemali, kodwa ngumsebenzi wesizwe ocwangcisiweyo wokuphumeza imveliso engenandleko.

Umhlathana 4. Ukupheliswa kweSigunyaziso. Isigunyaziso sikaRulumente sophuhliso nolawulo siphelele kwixesha langoku, kodwa ngokwendalo yaso sinesiphelo ngexesha. Siphela ngokuzenzekelayo kanye kanye ngomzuzu iLuphu eZimeleyo (AAASR) ivaliweyo kwaye ipaki yoomatshini ifikelele kumthamo wolawulo olupheleleyo, oluziphindaphindayo, noluzimeleyo. Kude kube lelo nqanaba, uRulumente uthwele uxanduva olupheleleyo, olusemthethweni lwengqiqo yobukrelekrele kunye noxolo lwabemi.


§7.  Ukwandiswa kweMveliso kaRulumente kunye nokuPheliswa kweMarike yeNzuzo

Umhlathana 1. URulumente uya kuqalisa ukwakhiwa okukhulu komthamo wemveliso ozenzekelayo ngokupheleleyo (AAASR). Yonke imithetho ekhoyo yokhuphiswano, izivumelwano zorhwebo, kunye nemigaqo yemarike ethintela okanye inciphisa icandelo likarhulumente ekwakheni, ekubeni ngumnini, nasekusebenziseni ezi ziseko zophuhliso iyatshitshiswa ngempembelelo ekhawulezileyo.

Umhlathana 2. IPaki yooMatshini eqhutywa nguRulumente nezizingabanini bazo iya kusasaza zonke izinto ezifunekayo eluntwini kunye neenkonzo simahla ebantwini. Nangona kunjalo, oku akusikeli mda ilungelo likaRulumente lelongamo lokuhlutha okanye ukufunyanwa ngenkani kweziseko zophuhliso zabucala kunye nezibonelelo kwiimeko apho umdla wesizwe ufuna ukuthathwa kwamandla ngokukhawuleza.

Inkcazelo: Lo mthetho wakhiwe ngobuchule kangangokuba uRulumente ngokuyintloko akunyanzelekanga ukuba ahluthe okanye agqibele kwiimbambano zomthetho neenkampani zabucala. Ngokuba uRulumente ezakhela ezakhe iziseko zophuhliso kwaye anike yonke into simahla, imarike yenzuzo ishiywe ingenaso isiseko sezoqoqosho kwaye ifa ngokupheleleyo ngokwendalo. Imali eyinkunzi yabucala ngaloo ndlela iyahluthwa amandla okulawula unikezelo lwelizwe, kodwa uRulumente ugcina ulawulo olupheleleyo lobundlobongela kunye nelungelo likaxakeka lokuthatha amashishini ukuba angazama ukonakalisa inguqu.

    • Ingqiqo kukuba uRulumente uyakha, imarike iyatsha, kwaye abantu bafumana ulongamo lwabo.



ISENZO SESITHATHU: UBUNINZI OBUPHELELEYO



§8.  UMgaqo-siseko weNtlanganisela kunye nePaki yooMatshini eQhelekileyo

Umhlathana 1. Isiseko sezoqoqosho nezobuchwepheshe zoluntu sixhomekeke kuMgaqo-siseko weNtlanganisela. Ngokwenziwa kwerobhothi ngokupheleleyo nangokungathelelanisiyo kuzo zonke iindawo zemveliso, uRulumente uya kuqinisekisa inkululeko epheleleyo yabemi bakhe.

Umhlathana 2. Ukwenziwa kwerobhothi ngokungaphelelanga ngokwenjenje kuchazwa njengenkohliso yengqiqo neyobuchwepheshe kwaye kubhengezwe njengokungahambelaniyo nomgaqo-siseko wesizwe.

Inkcazelo: Esi sishwankathelo sidweba umgca ocacileyo phakathi kwenkqubo endala nenkqubo entsha. Ngokuvalwa ngokomgaqo-siseko "ukwenziwa kwerobhothi ngokungaphelelanga," kuvala ucango kuthelelaniso lwexesha elizayo apho abezopolitiko okanye umdla okhethekileyo banokuzama ukushiya amacandelo athile ezandleni zenkqubo yamatyala abucala ngezizathu ezahlukeneyo. Inkululeko inamacala amabini—nokuba uluntu lukhululekile, okanye luyalawulwa.


§9.  IsiQinisekiso soBuninzi obuPheleleyo kunye noMgangatho

Umhlathana 1. Injongo ekwabelwana ngayo nguRulumente nabemi bakhe kukuqinisekisa, ngomanyano lolungiso, ubuninzi obupheleleyo bezinto ezibonakalayo nezenkcubeko obukowona mgangatho uphakamileyo onokucingwa kubo bonke abemi.

Umhlathana 2. Ipaki yerobhothi yesizwe kunye nobukrelekrele bayo bokwenziwa obunxulumeneyo benza umthombo ophilayo, ekwabelwana ngawo, nongenakuhluthwa. Lo mthombo uya kuhlala uphuhliswa ukuhlangabezana nezona mfuno ziphezulu zobugcisa nezokusebenza zoluntu, kwaye akufuneki uvunyelwe ukuba uye ugcwale ube ngumgangatho ongwevu, omncinci, okanye osikelwe umda.

Inkcazelo: Esi sishwankathelo sisiqinisekiso somgangatho somanyano. Sisusa isicupho sembali apho imveliso kaRulumente inxulunyaniswa nobuncinane obungwevu, obungenamphefumlo. Ngokubhala ukugqwesa kobugcisa kunye nezinto ezihlala ngonaphakade kumgaqo-siseko, ipaki yoomatshini iyanyanzelwa ukuba ihlale ikwincam yophuhliso. Oku kwenza kube yinto engenakwenzeka kubadlali babucala ukuba boyise uqoqosho olukhululekileyo besebenzisa uyilo olubhetele okanye inqanaba eliphezulu.


§10.  UKhuselo ngokuchasene noMsebenzi oNyanzelekileyo wezoQoqosho

Umhlathana 1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko epheleleyo kubukhoboka betyala obufihliweyo okanye obuvulelekileyo.

Umhlathana 2. Ekubeni ubuninzi bobuchwepheshe nobuzenzekelayo bubandakanya imveliso esisiseko kunye nemimandla yepremiyam nobunewunewu, naliphi na ilinge ngabadlali babucala lokuphinda bamisele izakhiwo zoqoqosho zokunyanzela ngokulawulwa kukodwa okanye ngokuxhaphaza ixesha lomntu liya kuthathwa njengelichasene nomgaqo-siseko.

Inkcazelo: Esi sisitshixo somthetho esichasene "neSiCupho soBunewunewu." Xa oomatshini bakaRulumente behambisa yonke into ukusuka kweyona ndidi incinci yengqolowa ukuya kweyona nto yobunewunewu iphambili simahla ngokupheleleyo, inkunzi ayisenaso nasiphi na isixhobo sokuphatha ezoqoqosho. Esi sishwankathelo senza kube lulwaphulo-mthetho ukuzama ukuxhaphaza umnqweno wabantu wemo okanye iimveliso ezizodwa ukubanyanzela ukuba babuyele ebukhobokeni bemivuzo. Usizi lwenkqubo yamatyala endala ngaloo ndlela lungcwatywa ngonaphakade.

    • Ingqiqo kukuba oomatshini bayagqitha, isicupho sobuwewunewu sityunyuzwa, kwaye inkululeko iba yinto epheleleyo.

Umhlathana 2. T.   UKUKHULULWA OKUKHULU KOLUNTU: Ukwala uMsebenzi njengeSidima – ULongamo njengeLungelo



Asisaliboni ilungelo loqoqosho lwetyala lokunyanzela abantu abaphilileyo kumsebenzi ongeyomfuneko. Ukwenza umsebenzi owenziwa ngumshatshini ngokukhawuleza nangcono asiyiyo igalelo eluntwini—kuyinkcitho yexesha lobomi bomntu kunye namandla okudala.

Ubunkokeli bezopolitiko banamhlanje busebenza njengeedinosaurs kwixesha ledijithali. Baxoxa ngeengxaki zayizolo ngeendlela zayizolo ngelixa betyeshela isixhobo sobuchwepheshe esele sikhona. Umgxeki uthi: "Ukwala umsebenzi njengesidima kuya kukhokelela kubemi abadandathekileyo, abangenasidima. Umsebenzi unika uluntu intsingiselo, ubuwena, kunye nolwakhiwo. Ngaphandle komzabalazo wokuphila, uluntu luya kutshona kwingxaki yentsingiselo."

Impendulo yoMbhalo: Oku kugxeka kwakhelwe phezu kwempazamo yenkolo yamatyala amadala yokuba uluntu lunemveliso kuphela phantsi kwesoyikiso sokufa yindlala yemali. ISabelo seLongamo asithengi ukungasebenzi—sithenga inkululeko.

Xa inqanawa yoomatshini iphakamisa idyokhwe yomsebenzi wemihla ngemihla kunye nokusebenza nzima okunyanzelekileyo, uluntu alushiywa kwisithuba. Sinikwa ixesha lokuzinikela kubugcisa, ezemidlalo, inzululwazi, ifilosofi, ukuhlola indalo, kunye nobudlelwane babantu obusulungekileyo. Uluntu aluyeki ukuyila; siyayeka nje ukusinda size ekugqibeleni siqale ukuphila. IXesha loMatshini ayisosiphelo somzamo woluntu, kodwa sisiqalo solongamo lwethu lokwenyani.
