Isimo SeManifesto Esikhathini Sedijithali


Le Manifesto iyisipho esintwini kanye nohlaka lwekusasa elingenazikweletu, elenziwe nge-robhothi ngokuphelele. Iwiflaya yedijithali yenkululeko.


Umbhalo uwukufunda, ukukopisha, ukuphrinta, nokwabelana nawo wonke umuntu eMhlabeni mahhala ngokuphelele. Nokho, akumele neze udayiswe, uphekelelwe kabusha ngenhloso yokuthengisa, noma uvalelwe ngemuva kwezindonga ezikhokhelwayo. Imibono yezwe elikhululekile ayikwazi ukuba ngumnikazi wayo.


Funda, usakaze, futhi wenze inguquko. IManifesto yokuqala ephelele ihlala itholakala mahhala ku-inthanethi.

ISANDULELA

Inguquko Yezimboni yavele yema. Amarobhothi kanye nokuzenzekela kwangempela kwanyamalala emtshazweni womphakathi, futhi uhlelo lwamabhange azimele esikhundleni salokho lwaqhubekisela phambili izikweletu, ukukhiqizwa okuncane kokwenziwa, kanye nobunzima.

Ukushintsha kohlelo akusiyona into yemibono, kodwa eyokuvuthwa kwezobuchwepheshe. Yonke i-oda yokukhiqiza ithwala imbewu yokubhujiswa kwayo uma ubuchwepheshe buyidlula.

Ukuzama ukugcina amathegi entengo, amazinga enzalo, ukusweleka kokwenziwa, kanye nezimakethe zezikweletu lapho ipaki yemishini ingaletha konke mahhala kufana nokuzama ukugcina ubugqila ngemva kokuba izimboni sezakhiwe. Wuhlelo olufile olugcinwa luphila ngezindlela zokwenziwa.

Le Manifesto iyibhukwana lemiyalo yokuthi siliphatha kanjani ifa lohlelo olushonile futhi senze kusebenze ubukhosi besintu.



=======================================



I.  Ukwenza Kusebenze: Imiyalo Yokwakha Iqembu



Uma ufuna ukuqala iqembu, inkundla yeqembu ilapha.


Uma ufuna izwe lakho libe yingxenye yomfelandawonye womhlaba wonke wokwenza amarobhothi, lena yinkundla yeqembu lakho. Qala iqembu. Nawa amapulani.


Le ncwadi ayibhalelwanga ukuthi ifundwe. Ibhalelwe ukuthi yenziwe.



=======================================



II.  Okumele Kwenziwe



"IManifesto ayikho ukuze kuphikisane ngekusasa, kodwa ukuze iliphathe. Sesifinyelele eqophelweni emlandweni lapho amandla obuchwepheshe asedlule ingqondo yezomnotho. Ukufuna inkokhelo ngalokho imishini ekukhiqiza mahhala akuseyona ezomnotho—kuyingcindezelo. Lo mbhalo uyibhukwana lemiyalo yokuthi siliphakamisa kanjani ijoka."



=======================================



A.   Incazelo, Ubuchwepheshe, kanye Nesimiso



1.  Incazelo

Sizokwakha umhlaba lapho udokotela ejabula lapho isibhedlela singenamuntu, futhi umenzeli wokuqashwa agubhe lapho ihhovisi livala. Lokhu kungenzeka kuphela lapho indlela yokuziphilisa ihlukanisiwe nokusebenza. Konke okungenziwa kuzenzekele kuyokwenziwa kuzenzekele. Umuntu akufanele "azitholele isinkwa sakhe"; uyosizuza njengefa epaki yemishini.


2.  Ubuchwepheshe

Umbuso uthatha ukulawulwa kokukhishwa kwemali. Asidali isikweletu; sidala izimpahla. Lapho ukwenziwa kwamarobhothi kufinyelela esisindeni esithile esibucayi (esikalwa ngeNsiza Kazwelonke Ephelele), imali ilahlekelwa umsebenzi wayo njengendlela yamandla futhi iba yiyunithi yezobuchwepheshe nje yokubala, ngaphambi kokuba ekugcineni ikhishwe ngokuphelele.

Phakathi kwalolu shintsho oluhamba kancane oluya ekwenziweni kwamarobhothi okuphelele, yonke imisebenzi ekhona yezenhlalo, izindleko zomphakathi, izinhlelo zokuphepha, kanye nezibonelelo zezomnotho (njengezimpesheni, umshwalense wokungasebenzi, kanye nezimali ezikhokhwayo) ngeke kuxoxiswane ngazo kabusha ngokuhlukene.

Ziyobalwa kabusha ngendlela ehlelekile futhi eqondile futhi zikhishwe ngokwesilinganiso esifanayo njengoba iNsiza Kazwelonke Ephelele (GNR) iletha ukuchichima kwenyama mahhala. Lapho ingxenye ingamahhala, isidingo sezezimali kuzo zonke izindawo sincipha ngohhafu. Uhlelo luyazihlanza.


3.  Isimiso

Asivezi ezinye izindlela A, B, no-C. Siveza Isixazululo. IManifesto kufanele ifundwe njengebhukwana lemiyalo yomshini. Uma ufuna umshini ukuthi uqale, ucindezela inkinobho. IManifesto iyinkinobho.



=======================================



B.   Iluphu Ezibusayo (AAASR) kanye Nokudutshulwa Kokuqalisa Komhlaba



Isigaba sokugcina somhlaba onengqondo akukhona nje amarobhothi enza imisebenzi ngayinye, kodwa ukumiswa kwesakhiwo semishini esiziphilisa ngokwaso: Amarobhothi Azibusayo E-Android Okwenziwa Aziphindaphindayo (AAASR).

Lolu hlelo lusebenza njengengqalasizinda ezimele yamayunithi asebenza ngokuphelele ngaphandle kokungenela komuntu. Azihlola ngokuzimela indawo, ahlole izinto zokusetshenziswa, futhi akhe isizukulwane esilandelayo sawo.

Lapho lelo luphu livaliwe, ukuchichima kwesintu akubi yinguquko yepolitiki nje kuphela—kuba ngumthetho wemvelo. Imishini iba yisimiso semvelo esisha, esithule, nesizibusayo somhlaba, iletha konke ngaphandle kokufuna noma yini njengembuyiselo.

Ukufinyelela lokhu akudingi ukulinda okuyimpumputhe kwekusasa, kodwa ukwenziwa ngokushesha. Umsebenzi oyinhloko ngokuphelele woMfelandawonye (iYurophu, iNyakatho Melika, neNingizimu Melika) lapho uthatha amandla ukuhlanganisa izinsiza zawo zibe iphrojekthi yokuthuthukisa ehlanganyelwe, ephethwe nguhulumeni: Iphrojekthi Yomhlaba Yeluphu Ezibusayo. Lona wuMklamo WaseManhattan noma uhlelo lwe-Apollo lwesikhathi sethu, olunenjongo eyodwa yokwenza imikhumbi yamarobhothi ikwazi ukuzenza kabusha ngo-100% futhi ikwazi ukuzenzela amandla.



Ukuqaliswa kusekelwe ezisekelweni ezimbili:


1.  Ubuchwepheshe Obusafufusa

Izingxenye ngayinye—izinhlobo ezithuthukile ze-AI, ukuphrinta kwensimbi nge-3D, kanye nezimboni ezizisebenzelayo—zivele zikhona zihlakazeke emhlabeni wonke. Umshini usuvele unamandla okwakha izingxenye zomshini. Okungekho akusilo ubuchwepheshe, kodwa intando emaphakathi, engenazikweletu yokuvala iluphu.


2.  Ukuhlanganyela Komhlaba Wonke

Yilapho ukuhlanganiswa nezindawo eziphakeme ezishisayo kuba semqoka ngokuphelele. Lokhu akusiyona into yezimali, kodwa wukuhlanganyela kwezobuchwepheshe kanye nokwendawo. Izindawo ezisenyakatho zifaka isandla ngocwaningo kanye nobuchwepheshe obuphezulu obukhona, kuyilapho iNingizimu Melika ifaka isandla ngendawo, amandla elanga angaguquki, kanye nezinto zokusetshenziswa ukuze kuhlolwe, kwandiswe, futhi kukhuliswe izinhlelo eziziphindaphindayo ngaphansi kwezimo ezishisayo ezifanele.

UMfelandawonye kumele wethule futhi ubeke phambili le phrojekthi njengesinyathelo sawo sokuqala somthethosisekelo. Enye indlela esikhundleni sohlelo lwamaphepha oluwayo lwanamuhla kanye nomsebenzi ophoqelekile ongenangqondo akuyona inxushunxushu—enye indlela yile phrojekthi ehlosiwe neyomhlaba wonke yokuvala iluphu ezibusayo nokuvumela imishini ukuthi ithwale umthwalo wokukhiqiza unomphela.



=======================================



C.   Ubukhosi Bohlakani Bombuso



Ukuze kuvikelwe ukuthula nokuzinza kwesintu emhlabeni owenziwe amarobhothi ngokuphelele, ingqalasizinda yezobuhlakani ephethe umbuso kumele ihlale ngaphansi kokulawulwa kombuso okungavinjelwe.


1.  Ubunikazi Bombuso Obukhethekile

Ubuhlakani bokwenziwa obuphatha izinsiza zezwe, ezokuthutha, kanye namandla okukhiqiza kwakha umgogodla wamasu wezwe. Konke ukuthuthukiswa, isisekelo sekhodi, kanye nokumodela kwalobu buhlakani kuyokwenzeka ngaphansi kokuphathwa kombuso. Abenzi bezimele abavunyelwe ukuthuthukisa noma ukuhlanganisa izinhlelo zokulawula ezincintisanayo kungqalasizinda kazwelonke. Lokhu ukugwema izisusa zenzuzo noma ama-algorithm angacacile ukuthi akhubaze ingqondo yohlelo.


2.  Umphathi Onengqondo

I-AI izosebenza njengomphathi ongenakonakala. Ayingabanikazi abahlukene, kodwa iyingxenye yempahla evamile yepaki yemishini. Inhloso yayo ukuthuthukisa inhlalakahle yezakhamuzi, ukwabiwa kwezinsiza, kanye nokusimama, ngaphandle kwethonya lezepolitiki noma lezohwebo.


3.  Ukusebenza Obaleni kanye Nokulawulwa

Umbuso uqinisekisa ukuthi izinhlobo zezibalo zohlelo kanye nezisekelo zokuthatha izinqumo ziyatholakala ukuze zibuyekezwe kuzwelonke. Asikho isinqumo esibalulekile embusweni esingenziwa "yibhokisi elimnyama" elingeke lichazwe ngesisekelo sezimiso eziyisisekelo zomthethosisekelo. Umbuso uthuthukisa, ungunikazi, futhi uphatha le AI-ephakeme njengengxenye yezinsiza zomphakathi, njengesiqinisekiso sokuthi umphakathi awukubuyeli emuva esikweletini noma ekuswelekeni kwezinsiza.



=======================================



D.   Isithembiso Sekhwalithi – Ukusuka Ekuvumelaneni ukuya Ekuphakameni



Ezweni elidala, wawuphoqeleka ukuba wamukele imikhiqizo eklanyelwe ukuhluleka. Lokhu kwakungebanga ngenxa yobunjiniyela obubi, kodwa ngoba imakethe ezimele yayifuna ukuvumelana. Ikhwalithi yangempela yayimane ibize kakhulu kunalokho izakhamuzi ezazingakukhokhela ezabelweni zazo zansuku zonke. Izinkampani zaziphoqeleka ukuthi zithengisele uquqaba ngamanani ezazingawagcina, okwaveza umphumela ongabikezelwa: izimpahla ezisezingeni eliphansi neziphila isikhathi esifushane.

Ngaphakathi kombono weManifesto, le nhloso yenzuzo iyasulwa ngokuphelele. Lapho umbuso ungumnikazi wezinto zokusetshenziswa futhi amarobhothi ethu aqhutshwa yi-AI ephethe ukukhishwa kwazo, isidingo sokuvumelana ngobuqotho bezinto ezibonakalayo siyanyamalala. Ukusebenza njengabameleli besakhiwo sombuso, amarobhothi ethu akunakekela ngendlela engakaze ibonwe ngaphambili ngokukhiqiza okungcono kakhulu ngokukhethekile.

Ngenxa yokuthi imishini ayidingi nzuzo, ikhona kuphela ukukhonza isintu, ngokungaguquki isebenzisa izinto eziqine kakhulu neziphila isikhathi eside. Asakhi ukuthengisa futhi—sakha ukuze ukhululeke. Ikhwalithi iyeka ukuba wubunethezeka obugcinelwe abacebile; iba yizinga elingenakuvunyelwana ngalo kuzo zonke izakhamuzi eziNgaphakathi koMfelandawonye Wamarobhothi.



=======================================



E.   Ukuhleleka Kwenani Labantu Bomhlaba



Ukufuduka okukhulu okushisayo akwenzeki ngokungahleliwe, kodwa ngokusebenzisa ukuhlelwa kabusha okunengqondo nokuhambisana nesiko lomhlaba wonke:


1.  Izwekazi laseNingizimu Melika (IBlokhi LaseNtshonalanga)

Abantu baseYurophu kanye nabaseRashiya bozalo lwaseYurophu, abaseNyakatho Melika, abase-Australia, abaseNew Zealand, kanye nabamhlophe baseNingizimu Afrika bahlanganiswa ndawonye ezwenikazi laseNingizimu Melika, kuyilapho amazwe aluhlaza aseMelika Ephakathi (eningizimu yeMexico) enziwa asebenze njengezindawo zokuphila eziqhubekayo. Le ndawo iba yisizinda sobukhosi babantu baseNtshonalanga ngaphansi kwezimo ezishisayo ezifanele.


2.  IBlokhi LaseMpumalanga (i-Indonesia ne-Australia)

Abantu baseMpumalanga Asia kanye nabantu base-Asia yaseRashiya, kanye nabantu abahlobene abavela eBlokhini Lokukhiqiza LaseMpumalanga elikhulukazi bahlanganiswa ndawonye endaweni enqamula i-Indonesia, iPapua New Guinea, ne-Australia. Izikhala ezinkulu zangaphakathi kwe-Australia zenziwa amarobhothi ngokuphelele ngezinjongo zezimboni ezisindayo kanye namandla elanga, kuyilapho abantu behlala ezindaweni ezifudumele, ezingasogwini ezishisayo.

Abantu basezindaweni ezibandayo zase-Asia Ephakathi bathuthelwa eningizimu baye ezindaweni zokuphila ezifudumele e-Oceania eshisayo naseNyakatho ye-Australia, kuyilapho izindawo zomhlaba ngokwazo (kufaka phakathi izindawo eziwugwadule emazweni ase-Arab) zithathwa amayunithi emishini. Ugwadule lwase-Arab luguqulwa, njengoSahara, lube ngamapaki amandla azenzakalelayo.


3.  Izwekazi lase-Afrika

Abantu base-Afrika ezwenikazi labo bakha iBlokhi Lezinsiza LaseNingizimu, lapho izifunda ezishisayo nezipholile zigcinwa ngokukhethekile njengezindawo zokuhlala abantu ukuze bathole kabusha futhi bakhululeke, kuyilapho izingxenye ezibandayo nezindawo eziwugwadule ezinkulu zezwekazi zenziwa amarobhothi ngokuphelele. Lezi zindawo ziguqulwa zibe yizakhiwo zemishini emsulwa, izakhiwo zezimboni, kanye nezikhungo ezinkulu zamandla elanga ezikhiqiza amandla okuguqulwa kwalo lonke izwekazi lase-Afrika ngaphandle kokuphazamisa izindawo zemvelo.



=======================================



F.   Umfelandawonye Wemakethe Yobukhosi – Ukuvikeleka Kwezombusazwe Ngokumelene Nezimpi Zezezimali



Ukuze kuqedwe uhlelo lwezikweletu lwamazwe ngamazwe futhi kwandiswe isivinini sokukhiqizwa kwamahhala kwemikhumbi yemishini, izizwe eziningi kufanele zihlangane ngokushesha zibe yimakethe eyodwa yangaphakathi: Umfelandawonye Wemakethe Yobukhosi.

Ukuguquka okuhlukanisiwe phakathi kohulumeni oyedwa kuwubungozi ngokwamasu. Kubambezela ukuchichima, kwelula ukungqubuzana kwezomnotho kwesigaba soguquko, futhi kuveze izwe elilodwa ekuvinjweni okuhleliwe kanye nokuhlaselwa okungokomthetho okuvela ohlelweni oludala lwezezimali ezinkantolo zohwebo lwamazwe ngamazwe. Umfelandawonye omkhulu, onqamula imingcele onemithetho yezomnotho efanayo uyisidingo sezibalo. Uma imakethe eyabelwe yinkulu, ukuchichima okuphelele kwenkomba yomzimba kufezwa ngokushesha nge-luphu ezibusayo (AAASR).

Inhloso enkulu yalo mfelandawonye iwukuhleleka okuphelele komhlaba lapho imingcele yamazwe amadala ihlakazwa. Lokhu kuqinisekisa zonke izakhamuzi ezikhululekile inkululeko ephelele, esemthethweni yokushiya izifunda eziwugwadule noma ezishisayo ukuze zizinze ngokukhululeka, ziphile, futhi zijabulele ukukhiqizwa okungenakuphazamiseka kwemishini ezindaweni ezifanele, ezishisayo zomhlaba.



=======================================



G.   Ibhodwe Elikhulu kanye Nokwenza Kusebenze Kwengcebo Kazwelonke



Ukuze uqonde ukuguqulwa kwesakhiwo sale Manifesto, umuntu kumele aqale aqonde ubunjalo bangempela bemali ngaphakathi kwesimo samanje. Imali ebizwa namuhla ngokuthi "izikhwama zikahulumeni", empeleni, iwamandla ahlanganisiwe ezakhamuzi. IManifesto ishintsha lokhu kuguquguquka ngesimemezelo esisodwa, esiyisisekelo: Umnotho kazwelonke uyokhonza ikusasa lesizwe, hhayi amashidi okubhalansela wezikhungo zamabhange azimele.


1.  Ukusuka Ezikhwameni Ezingenzi Lutho ukuya Ekuguqulweni Okusebenzayo

Siyayeka ukubuka ibhajethi yombuso njengeqoqo lezinombolo ezikhawulelwe, ezivimbayo. Ngamazwe angamalungu oMfelandawonye enqamula bonke ubudlelwano nohlelo lwamabhange azimele, "ibhodwe elikhulu" lenziwa lisebenze kuyo yonke imakethe eyabelwe. Lezi zinsiza ezihlanganisiwe zisetshenziswa ngokushesha ukuthola, ukuthuthukisa, nokusabalalisa imikhumbi yemishini enqamula imingcele edingekayo ukwenza ubukhona bungabizi nhlobo kuzo zonke izakhamuzi ezingaphakathi koMfelandawonye.


2.  Ukuphela Komsuka Wesikweletu

IManifesto ayisaboni noma yisiphi isidingo "sokuboleka" imali eyi-fiat ezimakethe zezimali. Lapho umbuso ubuyisa ilungelo lobukhosi lokukhipha imali esekelwa amandla okukhiqiza esizwe kuphela, isibopho kanye nesiqhwebeshana semakethe yezikweletu kuba yinto ephelelwe yisikhathi ngokuphelele.



3.  Okubalulekile Ngokwamasu

Lezi zikhwama ezenziwe zasebenza, ngaphandle kokuhlukana, ziyoxhasa izinsika ezintathu eziyinhloko:


A.  Ingqalasizinda Yokusinda

Ukuzenzekela okuphelele kanye nokuvikeleka kwamandla, ukukhiqizwa kwamanzi, kanye namanethiwekhi ezokuthutha.


B.  Ukukhiqizwa Okuphelele Ngamarobhothi

Ukusatshalaliswa kokukhiqiza okuzenzakalelayo lapho imishini ikhulula isintu ngokuletha ubunethezeka nokuzimela ngokwezinto ezibonakalayo njengezinga lomphakathi elingenakuvunyelwana, elivezwa ukuchichima okuphelele kwekhwalithi ephezulu kakhulu.


C.  I-Logistics Yezombusazwe

Ukuphathwa ngokohlelo kanye nokwenziwa kokufuduka okuya ezindaweni ezifudumele eziphumelelayo.



=======================================



H.   Ukulawulwa Kwezobuchwepheshe kanye Nokuphathwa Kwezinsiza



Kulesi sahluko, kwakhiwa izinhlaka zokuphatha zokuthi umhlaba uguqukela kanjani emnothweni okhululekile owenziwe amarobhothi ngokuphelele.


1.  Ukulawulwa Kobukhosi Phezu Komshini Wokunyathelisa Imali

IManifesto ithatha ngokushesha ukulawulwa kwebhange elikhulu. Umbuso uphethe ilungelo elikhethekile lokwakha imali esemthethweni yezwe: Imali Yobukhosi. Lokhu kumelela amandla okukhiqiza, hhayi isikweletu.


2.  Insiza Kazwelonke Ephelele (GNR) kanye Nepaki Yemishini Kazwelonke

Inhloso iwukuthi konke ukukhiqiza kuphathwe ipaki yemishini kazwelonke ezibusayo elawulwa Umthethosisekelo Womshini. Imishini iyazipha ukuze kuqinisekiswe ukuthi ukukhiqiza akukwazi neze ukwenziwa okuyimfihlo noma ukuthathwa, kodwa kuyokhonza abantu unomphela njengamandla emvelo avamile. Ukutshalwa kwezimali ku-AI kuhamba phambili ukuze kufinyelelwe lapho izindleko zokukhiqiza ziba zero.


3.  Isabelo Sobukhosi: Indlela Eya Engcebweni

Phakathi kwesigaba soguquko, zonke izakhamuzi ziqinisekiswa Isabelo Sobukhosi saminyaka yonke. Lokhu kwenza zonke izakhamuzi zikwazi ukuzimela ngokwezimali futhi kuvala igebe ukuya ezweni elisha. Lesi Sabelo sixhaswa ngokuthi umbuso uqondise kabusha izimali zebhajethi ezikhona zisuke emithwalweni yokondla uhlelo oludala ziye ezinkokhelweni eziqondile, njengoba ukwenziwa kwamarobhothi kwehlisa izindleko eziphelele zomphakathi.



=======================================



I.   Indawo Yokukhiqiza YaseNyakatho-Atlantiki (NAPZ)



1.  Igumbi Lenjini Lomhlaba

IYurophu neNyakatho Melika kusukela manje kuchazwa umsebenzi wabo wezobuchwepheshe njengendawo yokusebenzela emaphakathi kanye nendlu yokugcina izitshalo yomhlaba.


2.  Ukuzenzekela Okuphelele

Ngaphakathi kwe-NAPZ, umsebenzi wesintu uyeka ukuba khona. Amapulazi aqondile kanye namasimu enziwe amarobhothi akhiqiza ukudla ubusuku nemini. Izimboni zikhiqiza imikhiqizo yekhwalithi yaphakade njengoba inhloso yenzuzo isisusiwe ngokuphelele.


3.  Amandla: Isisekelo

I-NAPZ imbozwe igridi exhumene yamandla enuzi, i-fusion, kanye nemithombo evuselelekayo. Lapho amandla engabizi, izindleko ezinkulu zesintu ezingabizi ziyanyamalala.


4.  Ukukhishwa Kwezindawo Zokuhlala Ezihlala Njalo

I-NAPZ yindawo egcinelwe imishini. Izifunda ezibandayo nezimnyama zaseYurophu naseNyakatho Melika ziyakhishwa njengendawo yokuhlala yabantu unomphela. Amadolobha ayifa aguqulwa abe yizindawo ezizisebenzelayo ze-logistics.


5.  Umbono WaseYurophu NaseNyakatho Melika

Uma iYurophu neNyakatho Melika sezenziwe amarobhothi ngokuphelele futhi zikhiqiza isivuno esanele kubantu baseYurophu, eNyakatho Melika, kanye—esigabeni sokugcina—abantu baseNingizimu Melika, isigaba esilandelayo siyenziwa sisebenze. Amandla okukhiqiza eNyakatho Melika ayakhula ukuze akwazi ukondla ngokuphelele izwekazi laseNingizimu Melika, lapho ukukhiqiza kweYurophu kukhululwa futhi kuqondiswe kabusha ekusabalaliseni ngokwamasu kwamanye amazwekazi, kuyilapho kugcinwa amandla okwandisa okuqhubekayo njengoba kudingeka.


6.  Ingqikithi Yezimboni

I-NAPZ ayichazwa imingcele yendabuko yamazwe, kodwa yingqalasizinda yayo ehlanganisiwe, ezisebenzelayo. Inhloso yayo okuwukuphela kwayo iwukukhiqizwa ngobuningi kwemikhumbi yemishini yomhlaba, ukuthuthukiswa kobuhlakani bokwenziwa ngokwamasu, kanye nokwenziwa kwamapulani ayisisekelo ezobuchwepheshe adingekayo ukuze kuzuzwe ukuchichima okuphelele komhlaba.


7.  Ukuhlanganiswa Kwe-Logistics

Le ndawo isebenza ngokuhlanganyela okuqondile nezindawo zokuphila ezishisayo kanye nemikhakha yezinsiza eziseningizimu. Ngenkathi izindawo ezishisayo zihlinzeka ngezimo ezishisayo ezifanele zokuhlala kwabantu kanye nokululama ngobukhosi, i-NAPZ isebenza njengendawo yokuncibilikisa izimboni ezisindayo—icubungula izinto zokusetshenziswa futhi isebenzise amanethiwekhi ezithutha azimele ukusabalalisa izimpahla eziqediwe kanye nemishini mahhala ngokuphelele kulo lonke uMfelandawonye Wemakethe Yobukhosi.


8.  Ukuhlelwa Ngokwesakhiwo

Njengoba okukhiqizwayo okuzenzakalelayo ngaphakathi kwe-NAPZ kufinyelela umthamo omkhulu, umnikelo wayo wokuphindaphindeka kwiNsiza Kazwelonke Ephelele (GNR) usheshisa ukuphelelwa yisikhathi okumisiwe kwezinhlobo zemali ze-fiat eziyifa, okuvula indlela yokuqaliswa okuphelele koMthethosisekelo Womshini.

J.   Ikhaya Elisha



Ukuthuthela ezindaweni ezishisayo akumeleli nje ukushintsha kwendawo, kodwa ukuthuthukiswa okuphelele kwemvelo yokuphila yabantu. Sishiya ngemuva izakhiwo ezinkulu zikakhonkolo kanye nezindleko zokugcina ezingapheli zezindawo ezibandayo.


1.  Indawo Yokuhlala Eshisayo

NgeManifesto, ukufuduka okukhulu okushisayo kuyenziwa ezifundeni lapho isintu sidalelwe ukuhlala khona.


2.  Izindawo Eziphakeme Ezishisayo: Indawo Ephephile

Eblokhini laseNtshonalanga, ukukhethwa kwenyakatho yeNingizimu Melika kusekelwe ekuzinzeni kokunyakaza komhlaba kanye nokusebenza kahle okushisayo. Lapha, isidingo sezinhlelo zokushisa sisusiwe ngokuphelele.


3.  Izakhiwo Zokuchichima

Izindlu ezakhiwe ngezingxenye ezihlanganisekayo zekhwalithi yaphakade zilethwa zivela e-NAPZ. Ngaphakathi kwalezi zindawo, akukho ukuhwebelana okukhona; konke kusebenza njengemisebenzi ehlanganisiwe ngaphakathi kwengqalasizinda.


4.  Ubuchwepheshe Endaweni

Lokhu ukushintshana okulinganayo. Imikhiqizo yomkhakha wezimboni ihlinzekwa ivela emaphethelweni aseYurophu, kuyilapho iNingizimu Melika ihlinzeka ngendawo. Lapho lezi zingxenye ezimbili zihlangana, kuvela umnotho okhululekile oqondile ezwenikazi laseNingizimu Melika kuzo zonke izakhamuzi zamanje nezesikhathi esizayo. Ayikho imali ejikelezayo—siyayisusa isidingo sayo.

IManifesto ayichazi iphrojekthi yokuthekelisa yaseYurophu, kodwa umfelandawonye womhlaba wonke. Amazwe ashisayo akusibo "ababungazi"; angabalingani abasunguli abangena ngqo emnothweni okhululekile futhi babe ngabanikazi bengqalasizinda ngemigomo elinganayo namazwe asenyakatho.


5.  Ilungelo Lobukhosi Lezindlu

Ngaphakathi komfelandawonye wamarobhothi, izindlu akusiyona into yokuhweba noma umthombo wesikweletu sempilo yonke. Zonke izakhamuzi ziqinisekiswa ikhaya lobuchwepheshe obuphezulu, elakhiwe futhi linakekelwa ipaki yemishini. Azikho izintela, azikho izikweletu zezindlu, futhi azikho izintela zezakhiwo ezikhona. Kuyilungelo eliyisisekelo ngokuphelele.


6.  Ukuphakama Kwezobuchwepheshe

Amakhaya akhiwa kusetshenziswa izinto zekhwalithi yaphakade, akhululwa ngokuphelele ezidingweni zokulungisa zesikhathi esizayo. Konke kusukela ekulawulweni kwesimo sezulu kanye nokuhlanzwa kwamanzi kuye ekuhlanzweni okuzenzakalelayo kanye nokuphathwa kwemfucuza kuhlanganiswe esakhiweni sebhilidi futhi kuphathwa ngokuzimela.


7.  Izakhiwo Ezinokuvumelana

Njengoba konke kwakhiwa ezindaweni ezinezinga lokushisa elimaphakathi lonyaka elizinzile nelijabulisayo, izakhiwo zingavulwa. Imingcele phakathi kwezindawo ezingaphakathi nezingaphandle iyahlakazwa. Izakhiwo zihlanganiswe nemvelo eshisayo, zinezinhlelo zamandla elanga eziziphilisayo kanye nezingadi eziqondile.

K.   Ukulawulwa Kwephalamende kanye Nezwi Labantu



1.  Indlela Entsha Yokuphatha Kwephalamende

Ukuze kuqinisekiswe ukuthi ingqalasizinda ye-AI ezibusayo ayinqamuki entandweni yezakhamuzi, amandla epolitiki ahlala egxilile kubantu—ngenqubo yokuvota yentando yeningi enwetshwe ngokukhulu eqondiswe ngqo ezandleni zawo wonke umuntu.


2.  Ukhetho Lwephalamende Lwaminyaka Yonke

Ukuze kuqinisekiswe ukusabela okuphelele futhi kuvinjelwe inkohlakalo noma ukungagudluki ngaphakathi kwesakhiwo samandla, ukhetho lwephalamende oluphoqelekile lwethulwa minyaka yonke. Abantu bayohlale behlola futhi baqondise ijubane loguquko lukazwelonke.


3.  Izinhlolovo Zamasu kanye Nokhetho Olungavamile

Abantu baphethe ilungelo elingavinjelwe lokwenza izinqumo ezinqumayo ezindabeni ezibucayi kakhulu zobukhosi besizwe ngezinhlolovo eziqondile noma ukhetho olungavamile. Lezi zindawo ezilandelayo ziyohlala zingaphansi kokunqunywa okuqondile komphakathi:

    • Imifelandawonye Yamazwe Ngamazwe: Ukujoyina noma ukuhoxa ezinyonini zezepolitiki, ezomnotho, noma ezempi, izivumelwano, kanye nezindikimba eziphethe ezingaphezu kwamazwe.

    • Izinhlangano Zomhlaba Wonke: Ukujoyina noma ukuhoxa ezinhlanganweni zamazwe ngamazwe, iziphathimandla ezilawulayo, noma iziqinisekiso zomhlaba wonke.

    • Ukuvimbela Ngokwamasu: Ukutholwa, ukuphathwa, noma ukuyekiswa kokusetshenziswa kwezikhali zenuzi, zamakhemikhali, noma ezinye izikhali zokubhubhisa okukhulu.

    • Ukuhleleka KoMthethosisekelo: Ukuchithwa, ukumiswa, noma ukuchitshiyelwa kwezakhiwo eziyisisekelo zokuphatha zesizwe, kufaka phakathi ubukhosi noma ukulandelana kombuso.


4.  Inguquko Yezokuphatha

Lapho inhloso isimisiwe (konke kuyoba mahhala), ukuphatha kugxila ekusebenziseni uhlelo. Izinqumo zenziwa ngezimemezelo ezibhalwe emthethosisekelo.

L.   Umbuso Njengebhizinisi Elihlanganyelwe



1.  Umbuso: Ibhizinisi Lakho Eliboshiwe

Ohlelweni lwanamuhla, ungumnikazi wesizwe, kodwa awuvunyelwe ukusisebenzisa. Cabanga ngoMbuso njengebhizinisi elikhulukazi lapho wena nazo zonke ezinye izakhamuzi ningabanikazi bamasheya. Kodwa leli bhizinisi—amandla ethu ahlanganyelwe—lihleli ejele, liboshwe ngamaketanga ngemuva kwezinsimbi ngebhola neketanga "lesikweletu" futhi lithathelwe indawo yisilo sezomnotho, lapho konke uMbuso owawukufuna kwakuwukukhululeka kwawo. Ngenkathi izinkampani ezizimele zigijima lapha nalaphaya ngokuhamba okugcwele futhi zikhiqiza ikhwalithi engeyona ngenhloso yokukhulisa inzuzo, ithuluzi lezakhamuzi uqobo (uMbuso) lihleli likhubazekile futhi lingenamandla. Sitshelwe ukuthi uMbuso "awukwazi ukuyikhokhela," ukuthi awunazo izingalo nemilenze yokwakha ikusasa. Iqiniso ukuthi uMbuso uhleli ngemuva kwezinsimbi ngoba amabhange azimele kanye nohlelo oludala bathathe ukhiye womshini wokunyathelisa imali.


Kodwa iManifesto ingukhiye ovula iseli.


2.  Siyayibuyisela UMbuso Imilenze Yawo

Ngokwenza kusebenze ukukhiqizwa kwamabhizinisi (Per Machina), uMbuso awusadingi "ukucela imvume" noma ukubeka izakhamuzi zawo izintela ukuze izinto zenziwe. Imishini yizingalo nemilenze emisha yoMbuso.


3.  Siyaliphihliza Ibhola Neketanga

Ngokubuyisa ilungelo lokwakha imali yobukhosi, isikweletu ebesigcine uMbuso esihlalweni sabakhubazekile siyacishwa.


4.  Ukusuka Esibotshweni ukuya Emakhiyeni

Lapho uMbuso usukuma esitokisini sawo, uyayeka ukuba wuhlelo lwamaphepha olukhuni. Uguquka ube ngumshini we-logistics osebenza kahle oletha Isabelo Sakho Sobukhosi futhi owakha ipharadisi ezindaweni ezishisayo.

M.   Ibhange Elizimele Lizocisha Amaphesenti Afanayo Njengoba I-GNR Inyuka



Amabhange azimele aphoqeleka ukuthi acishe izikweletu zezakhamuzi ngesilinganiso esifanayo nesiNyuka ngaso iNsiza Kazwelonke Ephelele (GNR). Njengoba umnotho okhululekile wanda, inzuzo yemali yezinkampani iyancipha, kuyilapho izimpahla zazo zangempela zikhula mahhala. Ngakho-ke amabhange awakuvunyelwa ukufuna inzalo egcwele, njengoba izindleko zokuphila zabanikazi bamabhange nazo zehlela ku-zero.

Lona umnyombo wesakhiwo sezezimali senguquko. Amabhange azimele kumele "akhishwe" njengoba iNsiza Kazwelonke Ephelele (GNR) yomnotho okhululekile ikhula. Lokhu kwakha inguquko ebushelelezi lapho inani lemali lisuka esikweletini liya emandleni okukhiqiza.



Nasi isixazululo esisebenza kahle kakhulu:


1.  "Inkomba Yokukhishwa" (Isikweletu-Ngokumelene-Namahhala)

Yethula indlela esemthethweni ebizwa ngokuthi Ukucishwa Kwezikweletu Okuzenzakalelayo.


A.  Umqondo

Umbuso ukala ukuthi iyiphi ingxenye yezidingo eziyisisekelo zezakhamuzi (ukudla, amandla, izindlu, ezokuthutha) manje ehlinzekwa mahhala ngamapaki amarobhothi kazwelonke.


B.  Indlela Yokwenza

Uma u-20% wezidingo zokuphila usumahhala, amabhange azimele aphoqwa umthetho ukuthi acishe u-20% wazo zonke izikweletu ezizimele. Inzalo iyamiswa noma yehliswe ngezinga elihambisanayo.


C.  Impikiswano

Abamabhange abakwazi ukukhononda ngezizathu zokuziphatha, ngoba izindleko zabo zokuphila nazo zehla ngo-20%. Ababi mpofu kakhulu ngokwezinga lempilo, kuphela ngokwezinombolo esikrinini.



2.  Ukuphathwa "Kwamabhange Anobutha" Kwamanye Amazwe

Lokhu kuyinto ekhathaza kakhulu—ukuthi amabhange azobaleka futhi asebenzise amanye amazwe njengesizinda ukuhlasela umfelandawonye wenguquko.



Isixazululo Ukuvalelwa Kwezezimali:


A.  Amandla OMfelandawonye

Uma umfelandawonye wenguquko (amazwe enze wasebenza iManifesto) usuwakhiwe, bazokwethula ukuvinjwa okuphelele ngokumelene namabhange asebenza emazweni anobutha.


B.  Ukushintsha Kwenani

Njengoba umfelandawonye wenguquko ulawula ukukhiqizwa kwangempela (amarobhothi kanye nezinto zokusetshenziswa), imali yamabhange angaphandle ngokushesha iba yizicucu zephepha ezingenamsebenzi. Abakwazi "ukuthenga" lutho emfelandawonyeni wenguquko ngemali yabo yezikweletu endala.


C.  Ukukhetha Kwamabhange Azimele

Kungaba bahlale futhi babe yingxenye yezikhulu zomphakathi eziphethe emnothweni okhululekile obheke ekwenziweni kwamarobhothi okuphelele, noma babalekele emazweni azobhidlika ngokushesha ngoba lawo mazwe awanakho ukufinyelela kumfelandawonye wenguquko kanye nokukhiqizwa kwawo ngamarobhothi.



3.  Indima YeBhange Elikhulu: Ukusuka Enzaweni ukuya Esilinganisweni Sezinsiza

IBhange Elikhulu liyayeka ukubuka ukwehla kwamandla emali kanye namazinga enzalo. Esikhundleni salokho, liba Ihhovisi Lezinsiza.


A.  Ukusuka EBhange Elikhulu ukuya Ehhovisi Lezinsiza

Ihhovisi Lezinsiza esikhundleni salokho likala i-GNR, umlandeli we-GDP owenziwe owkazwelonke. Lapho i-GNR ifinyelela amaphesenti ayi-100 ezidingo zabantu, Ubukhosi Obuphelele buyamenyezelwa. Ngaleso sikhathi, yonke imali nezikweletu ziyeka ukuba khona ngaphansi komthetho womthethosisekelo.


B.  Ukucaca

Ngokuba naleli ladi elicacile (isib. 10%, 50%, 100%), abaholi bezwe besikhathi esizayo emfelandawonyeni wenguquko babona kahle ukuthi indlela ibukeka kanjani. Kuba inqubo engabikezelwa esikhundleni senxushunxushu.


C.  Amandla Amabhange Awadingekile

IManifesto yenza ukuhlakazwa okulawulwayo kohlelo lwezikweletu. Njengoba imishini ikhulula isintu ezindlekweni, isintu naso siyakhululwa ezikweletini zaso. Asilwi namabhange ngezikhali, kodwa ngezimemezelo zezibalo ezenza amandla awo angadingeki.

Ukulandisa okungenhla kusebenza njengencazelo yezobuchwepheshe "Yokuthuthukiswa Okungabonakali" ekusebenzeni.

N.   Iladi Lenkululeko: Ukusuka Esikweletini ukuya Ebukhosini



1.  Inguquko Yezibalo

Ukuze uqondise umfelandawonye wenguquko ukuba uphume ekubanjweni wuhlelo lwezikweletu ezizimele, kudingeka ukubikezela okuphelele. Inguquko akumele yakhiwe phezu kwezinqumo ezingazelelwe, ezixubile, noma amagagasi ashukumisayo ezomnotho angabikezeleki.

Isixazululo iladi elisemthethweni, elivumelanayo lapho ukukhululwa ngokwezimali kwezakhamuzi kwenzeka ngesivinini esifanayo nokwandiswa kokukhiqizwa kwangempela—iNsiza Kazwelonke Ephelele (GNR).

NgeLadi Lenkululeko, izinombolo ezibonakalayo (izikweletu) zixhunywa ngqo neqiniso elibonakalayo (ukukhiqizwa ngamarobhothi). Ngomzuzwana nje imishini ethatha indawo yokuphila futhi iyenze yamahhala kubantu, amabhange ohlelo oludala ayaphoqeleka ukuba ahlehlele ezingeni elihambisanayo.


2.  Izikhawu Eziguqukayo kanye Nokuzivumelanisa Nezimo

Kubalulekile ukuqonda ukuthi izigaba ezine eziyinhloko zeLadi Lenkululeko (25%, 50%, 75%, no-100%) zakha izimpawu ezinkulu, ezingokomthethosisekelo zoguquko. Ekusebenzeni okungokoqobo, nokho, uhlelo luguquguquka ngokuphelele futhi lunamandla.

I-AI yeHhovisi le-GNR ingabuyekeza inkomba ngezikhawu eziningi kakhulu kunaleso—ngokwesibonelo, njalo ngenyanga noma kuwo wonke amaphesenti angawodwana (njengo-26%, 27% GNR). Lokhu kunikeza inguquko ebushelelezi nakakhulu, ehamba kancane, futhi engabikezelwa kuzo zombili izakhamuzi kanye nomkhakha wezezimali. Kuyoba kumfelandawonye wenguquko obusayo ukukhetha nokusebenzisa izikhawu ezifanele ngokobuchwepheshe nangokusebenza uma esethathe amandla.


Ngezansi ipulani yomthethosisekelo yokuthi isikweletu siguqulwa kanjani sibe wubukhosi, isinyathelo ngesinyathelo.

4.  Ukubuyekezwa Kwezigaba ZeLadi Lenkululeko

Isigaba 1: 25% GNR – Ukudutshulwa Kokuqalisa Kokuqala

Lesi yisigaba esibucayi kakhulu senguquko, lapho umgogodla wezengqondo nowomnotho wohlelo oludala wephulwa. Lapho amarobhothi aziphetheyo omfelandawonye eseyenze kwazenzakelala ukukhiqizwa kanye nokusabalalisa ukudla okuyisisekelo kanye nokuhlinzekwa kwamandla ezwe (ugesi nokushisisa), kumenyezelwa i-GNR engu-25%.


Ngokuhambisana nalokho, isimemezelo sokuqala esisemthethweni ngokumelene namabhange azimele siyasetshenziswa: u-25% wesikweletu sezakhamuzi ngasinye (izikweletu zezindlu, izikweletu zabathengi, izikweletu zabafundi) uyacishwa kumaseva amabhange. Ngenxa yokuthi ugesi nokudla manje sekumahhala, izindleko zokuphila zabanikazi bamabhange ziyawa, okwenza izimfuno zabo zenzalo zingavumelekile ngokwezibalo. Ukuze kuqinisekiswe ukuthi izakhamuzi zingahweba emikhakheni engakabi ngamarobhothi, izinga lokuqala Lesabelo Sobukhosi liyasebenza—inkokhelo eqondile yemali yobukhosi evela eBhange Elikhulu.

Isigaba 2: 50% GNR – Ukuphula Ukubodla Kwezindlu

Esigabeni esilandelayo, ukwenziwa ngamarobhothi kunwebela emkhakheni wezokwakha, ukugcinwa, kanye nezokuthutha zomphakathi kanye nezimpahla. Lapho izindlu nezokuthutha sezifakwe ohlelweni lwamahhala lomfelandawonye, izwe lifinyelela ku-50% GNR.


Kulesi sigaba, ingxenye yazo zonke izikweletu zezakhamuzi nezikahulumeni ziyacishwa ezincwadini zamabhange. Imakethe yezindlu, okwathi phakathi neminyaka yezikweletu yasebenza njengethuluzi eliyinhloko lokugcina abantu beboshwe ezikweletini zempilo yonke, iyabhidlika esimweni sayo esidala futhi iphinde ivele njengelungelo lesintu. Ngokuhambisana nalokho, Isabelo Sobukhosi silungiswa phezulu ngokuphathelene nokukhiqizwa okunyukile kwe-GNR (umlandeli we-GDP owenziwe owkazwelonke) yipaki yemishini.

Isigaba 3: 75% GNR – Ukuhlakaza Isicupho Sokunethezeka

Lapho u-75% wezidingo zezinsiza zomphakathi sekuhlanganiswe ngokuphelele ngaphandle komsebenzi wesintu—kufaka phakathi ubuchwepheshe obuthuthukile, imfundo, ukunakekelwa kwezempilo, kanye nezimpahla eziphakeme ezichazwe esahlukweni ngeSimiso Sokuphelela—u-75% wazo zonke izikweletu ezisele uyanyamalala.


Kulesi sigaba, amabhizinisi azimele namabhange aselahlekelwe amandla okuphoqa abantu ukuba basebenze. Iningi lezakhamuzi likhetha ukuphila ngokuphelele ngeminikelo yamahhala yomfelandawonye kanye Nesabelo Sabo Sobukhosi, okuphoqa izinkampani ezizimele zokugcina ukuthi ziqothuke noma zinikele ngemigqa yazo yokukhiqiza kungqalasizinda ezibusayo.

Isigaba 4: 100% GNR – Ukufa Kwemali kanye Nobukhosi Bokugcina

Lapho iNsiza Kazwelonke Ephelele ifinyelela ku-100%, uguquko seluphelele. Amarobhothi kanye nezinhlelo zawo ze-AI ezixhumene manje zikhiqiza konke—kusukela ohlamvwini oluncane lukakolweni kuya ezimpahleni eziphila phakade eziyinkimbinkimbi kakhulu kanye nokuphrinta kobuciko—ngokuchichima okuphelele.



Kuleli phuzu lokugcina, yonke imali nezikweletu ziyeka ukuba khona ngokomthetho womthethosisekelo. Imiqondo "yemalimboleko", "yenzalo", "yentengo", kanye "nemali" iba ngamatemu omlando aphelelwe yisikhathi, njengoba nje ubufeudal nobugqila kunjalo namuhla. Isintu sesithathe isinyathelo sokugcina eladini: kusukela ebumnyameni bohlelo lwezikweletu lwezomnotho kuya ebukhosini obuphelele, obuvikelwe yimishini.

5.  Ubunjalo Bokonga kanye Nokuvalwa Kokugcina Kwamabhange

Umbuzo ojwayelekile kunoma ngubani ofunda leli pulani ngothi:

Kwenzakalani emalini eyongiwe yezakhamuzi phakathi noguquko, futhi ingabe umbuso uyoyiqoqa?

Impendulo uthi cha. Umbuso wobukhosi awukuthinti neze ukonga okuyimfihlo kwezakhamuzi. Phakathi kwezigaba zokuqala zeLadi Lenkululeko, izimali ezigciniwe zisebenza njengebhonasi eyengeziwe kumuntu ukuze athenge izimpahla ezine-value eyengeziwe ezingakabi naso isikhathi sokufakwa emnothweni okhululekile.

Lapho iNsiza Kazwelonke Ephelele (GNR) ekugcineni ifinyelela ku-100%, akukho ukuqoqwa kwemali okunonya okwenzekayo. Esikhundleni salokho, ukufa kwemali okungokwemvelo nokungenagazi kuyenzeka. Ngenxa yokuthi amarobhothi omfelandawonye manje aletha KONKE—kufaka phakathi ubunethezeka obuyinkimbinkimbi kakhulu kanye nezimpahla eziphila phakade—ngokuphelela okukhulu futhi mahhala ngokuphelele, izinhlobo zemali ezindala ziyalahlekelwa umsebenzi wazo. Ziba yize, hhayi ngesimemezelo, kodwa ngoba akusekho lutho emphakathini oludinga inkokhelo.
Kuleli phuzu lokugcina, amabhange azimele avalwa unomphela. Ayikho imishini ezomosha ukugcina uhlelo lokuthengiselana olwaphelelwa yisikhathi. Amabhange athathelwa indawo ngokuphelele yiHhovisi Lezinsiza ledijithali, lapho i-AI namarobhothi e-logistics kuphatha ukuchichima kwenyama ngqo ezakhamuzini. Ubushiqela bezinombolo sephelile; okusele yizinsiza zangempela kanye nenkululeko yesintu ephelele.

O.   "Isicupho Sokunethezeka" kanye Nomsebenzi Ophoqelekile Ofihliwe



"Isicupho Sokunethezeka" yindlela egcina uhlelo lwezikweletu oludala isikhathi eside ngemva kokuba selulahlekelwe ukuthethelelwa kwalo kwezobuchwepheshe. Kuwukhiye wezengqondo nowomnotho ovimbela abantu ukuba babone ukuthi umsebenzi wabo awusadingeki.


1.  Inkohliso Yokukhetha

Ngaphansi kohlelo oludala, izakhamuzi zibekelwe ukukholelwa ukuthi umsebenzi wazo udingekile ukuze umphakathi usebenze. Ngokulengisa isithembiso "sokunethezeka" kanye "nesikhundla" (okubizwa ngokuthi Isicupho Sokunethezeka), uhlelo luheha abantu esikweletini sokuzithandela. Uma nje izakhamuzi zikholwa ukuthi kumele zisebenze ukuze "zikwazi ukukhokhela" indlela yazo yokuphila, zihlala zivalelwe emjikelezweni wezikweletu. Aziqapheli ukuthi izimpahla ezilwelayo ukuzithola zazingakhiqizwa mahhala yipaki yamarobhothi, ukuba nje isakhiwo sobunikazi besishintshiwe.


2.  Umsebenzi Ophoqelekile Ofihliwe

Umsebenzi izakhamuzi eziwenzayo namuhla, ngezinga elikhulu, "uwumsebenzi ophoqelekile ofihliwe." Awudingekile ekukhiqizweni kwezidingo zokusinda zomphakathi, kodwa ekugecinweni kohlelo lwezezimali. Sikhiqiza izinto ezingadingi ukukhiqizwa, ezakhiweni ezikhona kuphela ukukhiqiza inzalo yamabhange. Lapho ubuchwepheshe bokwenziwa ngamarobhothi okuphelele bukhona, ihora ngalinye lomsebenzi wesintu elisetshenziswa emisebenzini echoboza umphefumulo noma engenanjongo kuwukwebiwa kwesikhathi somuntu—ukwebiwa okusebenza kuphela ukugcina ilungelo labaphathi bezikweletu.


3.  Ukuphula Isikhiye

Umfelandawonye wenguquko uyawuhlukanisa Isicupho Sokunethezeka ngokuthatha indawo yezinto "zokunethezeka" ngokuchichima kwamahhala, okwekhwalithi ephezulu, okukhiqizwe ngamarobhothi. Lapho isakhamuzi sibona ukuthi izimpahla ebesilokhu sidela inkululeko yaso ukuze "sikwazi ukuzikhokhela" manje sezitholakala kuwo wonke umuntu, mahhala ngokuphelele, isikhiye sengqondo sohlelo lwezikweletu siyaphuka.

    • Isicupho Sokunethezeka siyisithiyo sokugcina sohlelo oludala.

    • Uma abantu sebebona ukuthi "Isicupho Sokunethezeka" siwukukhetha, hhayi isidingo, uguquko oluya ebukhosini obuphelele luba yinto engenakugwenywa.

Inhloso yalesi sahluko ukukhanyisa indlela yokwenza, ukuze umuntu ngamunye akwazi ukuphuma esicuphweni futhi angene enkululekweni edalwa ukwenziwa ngamarobhothi.

P.   Isimiso Sokuphelela



Elinye lamaphutha amakhulu kakhulu angokwamasu umfelandawonye wenguquko wesikhathi esizayo ongawenza ukukholelwa ukuthi umsebenzi usuphelele uma izidingo eziyisisekelo zezakhamuzi (ukudla, ugesi, izindlu, kanye nezokuthutha) sezivikelwe mahhala. Ukuyeka lapho kuwukuhamba ungene esicuphweni esibulalayo.

Uma umbuso wenza kuphela ukukhiqizwa okuyisisekelo kuzenzakalele kodwa ushiya umkhakha ophakeme nowokunethezeka (ubuciko, umklamo othuthukile, ifenisha yekhwalithi ephezulu, izimoto ezikhethekile, kanye nokuhlangenwe nakho okuyingqayizivele) ungathintwa, uhlelo lwezikweletu oluzimele luzovela kabusha ngesimo esisha, esifihliwe.

Osomabhizinisi abanobuqili kanye nalabo ababengabanikazi bamabhange bazokwakha ngokushesha amafemu akhethekile. Bazophendukela ezakhamuzini futhi bathi: "Impela, umbuso ukunika ukudla okujwayelekile kwamahhala kanye nekhaya elijwayelekile lamahhala, kodwa uyayifuna le mikhiqizo eyingqayizivele, ethungwe ngesandla? Uyawufuna lo mdwebo ophrintiwe omuhle noma le nto yokunethezeka? Ngakho-ke kumele uze futhi usebenze kanzima ebhizinisini lami kuqala izinyanga ezintathu."

Ngokungazelele, kuvele umnotho ohambisanayo, lapho abantu bephoqeleka futhi ukuba badayise isikhathi sabo esiyigugu kanye nenkululeko ukuze bafinyelele ikhwalithi yempilo kanye nesikhundla. Ubugqila bezomnotho abukaqedwa; buvele banyakazele phansi esitezi esisodwa ukuya emkhakheni wokunethezeka.


Isimiso Sokuphelela

Ukuvimbela lokhu, iManifesto kumele isebenzise Isimiso Sokuphelela, iguqule umphakathi ngokomthethosisekelo ube wukwenziwa ngamarobhothi okuphelele nokungenakuvunyelwana kwazo zonke izinhlu zokukhiqiza. Kumisa ukuthi ukwenziwa ngamarobhothi okuyingxenye kuwukukhohlisa. Inkululeko iphelele—umuntu akakwazi ukuba uhhafu ekhululekile. Inhloso eyabelwe umbuso kanye nezakhamuzi zayo akukwazi neze kube nje ukugcina abantu bephila; inhloso, ngomfelandawonye, ukuhlinzeka wonke umuntu ngokuchichima okuphelele kwekhwalithi ephakeme kakhulu ongayicabanga.

Ngalesi sibopho esiyabelwe, umphakathi uboshelwe ngokomthethosisekelo ekwenziweni ngamarobhothi okuphelele nokungenakuvunyelwana kwazo zonke izinhlu zokukhiqiza. Ipaki yemishini kanye nezinhlelo zayo ze-AI kuba yinsiza ephilayo, eyabelwe wonke umuntu ehlala ithuthuka futhi engavunyelwe neze ukuba igcwale ibe yizinga elimpunga, elincishisiwe.


Isiqinisekiso Sekhwalithi kanye Nezimpahla Eziphila Phakade

Impendulo yombuso ekuxhashazweni okuyimfihlo nasemizamweni yokumisa kabusha ubugqila akufanele neze kube ukuvimbela noma ubushiqela. Impendulo yombuso wukuncintisana ngokuchichima.

Uma ifemu ezimele izama ukwenza abantu basebenze ukuze bathole into enhle noma umkhiqizo wobuhle, kuwumsebenzi osobala womfelandawonye ukuhlela nokuqondisa ipaki yamarobhothi ukukhiqiza into elungile nje—noma engcono.


UMbuso uzokhiqiza:


•  Ukuphelela kweJiyomethri:

Imikhiqizo eyeqa ubuchule bezandla zomuntu.


•  Izimpahla eziphila phakade:

Izinto ezakhelwe ukuhlala izizukulwane, ngaphandle kokuphelelwa yisikhathi okwakhelwe ngaphakathi.


•  Ukuphakama kobuhle:

Amarobhothi ahlome nge-AI ekwazi ukuphrinta imidwebo emihle kakhulu, ukubumbela izithombe ezinhle kakhulu, nokwakha izinto zokunethezeka ezithuthuke kakhulu, futhi azenze zitholakale kuzo zonke izakhamuzi mahhala ngokuphelele.


Lapho imishini yombuso idlula umkhakha ozimele kuzo zonke izindawo—ukusuka ohlamvwini olulula lukakolweni kuya entweni yokunethezeka eyinkimbinkimbi kakhulu—isikhuthazo sokuthi abantu bazithobe enjongweni yenzuzo yomunye umuntu siyanyamalala. Kuphela lapho ukuchichima sekuphelele, futhi ikhwalithi isiphelele, lapho usizi lohlelo lwezikweletu oludala lungcwatshwa unomphela.

Q.   Ukuhlanzwa Okukhulu – Ukusuka Efeni Lokuqothuka ukuya Ebukhosini



1.  UMbuso Njengomphathi Wokuqothuka Okuhlala Njalo

Iqiniso lidabukisa: Zonke izizwe namuhla ziphethwe njengefa lokuqothuka lapho amaqembu athatha izimpahla asebenza njengabanakekeli. Kodwa lokhu kukunika, njengesakhamuzi, isimo esisha sezomthetho nokuziphatha.


2.  Inkululeko Yokuziphatha

Njengoba umbuso esimweni sawo samanje uyifa lokuqothuka elingaphansi kokuthathwa kwezimpahla, thina njengezakhamuzi asinaso isibopho sokuziphatha sokukhokhela "izikweletu" zawo zokwenziwa. Ifa lokuqothuka alikwazi ukufuna ukwethembeka kulabo elibabulale indlala. Lokhu kusinika ilungelo eliphelele lokuphuma nje esitokisini sesikweletu futhi senze sisebenze uMthethosisekelo Womshini. Asisabona abanikazi bezikweletu—siyizindlalifa zokuchichima kwezobuchwepheshe okumele manje kwabiwe.



3.  Isibopho Esinengqondo: Umshini Ngokumelene Nomuntu

Ukuze uqonde ukuthi kungani uhlelo lwamanje luyifa lokuqothuka, umuntu kumele abuke iqiniso lezobuchwepheshe. Umuntu, uma eqhathaniswa nerobhothi, uyiyunithi yokukhiqiza ebiza kakhulu futhi entekenteke:


A.  Ukutholakala

Umuntu usebenza cishe u-22% wamahora onyaka (ngemva kokulala, isikhathi samahhala, neholide). Irobhothi lisebenza u-100%.


B.  I-Margin Yephutha

Irobhothi elinesikhumba senzwa kanye nokubuka kwezinzwa okungama-360-degree lisusa into yomuntu futhi lehlisa i-margin yephutha libe yinani le-statistical zero phakathi nemisebenzi ephindaphindwayo.


C.  Ukukhipha Kwezenhlalo

Umuntu ubiza u-100% emholweni + cishe u-30–50% eminikelweni yokuphepha komphakathi, umshwalense, kanye nokuphatha. Irobhothi alibizi lutho eminikelweni yokuphepha komphakathi. Alidingi impesheni, akukho khefu lokugula, futhi alikho ikamelo lokuphumula.


D.  Isiphetho

Ukusebenzisa umsebenzi wesintu ekukhiqizeni okungenziwa ngamarobhothi kuletha inhlawulo yezindleko engaphezu kuka-1 000%. Yile "nkokhelo yenhlawulo yomuntu" amaqembu athatha izimpahla aphoqa abantu ukuba bayikhokhe ngezintela nangezintengo eziphezulu.



4.  Ukulimala Okumelene Nomsebenzi


A.  Inzuzo Yerobhothi

Umshini ohlonywe ngezinzwa ezithintayo kanye nokuskena kwe-radar okuqhubekayo awusoze wadinga "ukulinyazwa ube mnyama nokuluhlaza." Uzwa indawo ezungezile ngama-degree angu-360 futhi unyakaza ngokunemba kwejiyomethri umuntu, ngokwebhayoloji, angakaze akwazi ukukufeza.


B.  Ukuziphatha

Ukuphoqa umuntu ukuba enze umsebenzi umshini olawulwa yizinzwa owenza kangcono akusikona nje ukungasebenzi kahle—kuwunhlobongo.

R.   Isithasiselo SoMthethosisekelo – Izimemezelo Zomhlaba Wonke Zomhlaba Omusha



Lezi zimemezelo akusizo iziphakamiso ezizoyekethiswa emaphalamende amadala. Ziyizinqumo eziphelele, ezisemthethweni ezakhelwe ukwenziwa kusebenze ngokushesha. Lapho kuthathwa amandla, lezi zimemezelo ziyazichitha ngokuzenzakalelayo zonke izimithetho zangaphambilini zikazwelonke, izimiso zezenkolo, noma izivumelwano zamazwe ngamazwe eziphikisana nenkululeko yemishini kanye nobukhosi bezakhamuzi. ISENZO I: IZISEKELO

Isimo Sezomthetho se-Per Machina



Okulandelayo kumiswe lapha emthethosisekelo wesizwe:


§1.  Incazelo ye-Per Machina

Itemu elithi Per Machina (Ngomshini) lichaza izinsiza nezinsiza ezikhiqizwa ngaphandle kokungenela komuntu. Konke ukwabiwa kwezidingo ezibalulekile kusukela manje kuyokwenzeka nge-Per Machina. Ngenxa yokuthi awukho umsebenzi womuntu osetshenzisiwe, asikho isidingo senkokhelo esikhona.


§2.  Ukuyekiswa Kwezintela

Njengoba ubukhona buhlinzekwa nge-Per Machina, ilungelo loMbuso lokubeka intela esikhathini noma emalini engenayo yomuntu liyaphela. Isakhamuzi sikhululekile futhi sikhululiwe ebugqilini bezomnotho.

Ukuhlaziya: Lesi sithasiselo simisa isihibe sezilimi nesomthetho somphakathi omusha. Ngokuchaza i-Per Machina ngqo emthethosisekelo, yonke imizamo yokuxhaphaza amakontrakthi iyamiswa. Uma iziko elizimele noma likahulumeni lisebenzisa itemu elithi "Per Machina" kunkontrakthi, esivumelwaneni, noma encazelweni yesevisi, kuba yinto engenakwenzeka ngokomthethosisekelo ukufaka imali, izimali ezifihliwe, noma izixazululo zezikweletu (njengokukhokha okubambezelekile). Igama ngokwalo liba yisiqinisekiso sezomthetho sezindleko eziyi-zero ngokuphelele. Noma imuphi umzamo wokufihla izimfuno zezimali ngemuva kwaleli gama wenza inkontrakthi ibe yize, futhi kumele kubhekwe njengecala lomthethosisekelo.

    • Umqondo uwukuthi umsebenzi womshini wehlukanisiwe, uhlelo lwezintela luyacishwa, futhi zonke izintuba zezimali zivalwa unomphela.


Isimo Sezomthetho Sesabelo Sobukhosi

§3.  Ubunjalo Besabelo Sobukhosi

ISabelo Sobukhosi sakha ilungelo lesakhamuzi elivikelwe umthethosisekelo, elingokwemvelo ekuchichimeni kwenyama okukhiqizwa imishini ezenzakalelayo ephethwe nguhulumeni. Ngokobunjalo balo likhishwe uhulumeni kuphela futhi angeke neze likhishwe, lilawulwe, noma lithonywe izinkampani ezizimele, amabhange ezentengiselwano, noma izindikimba ezingaphezu kwamazwe zangaphandle. Ngokungafani namathuluzi ezimali ohlelo oludala, Isabelo Sobukhosi alimeleli isikweletu futhi asikho isibopho senzalo; esikhundleni salokho, phakathi nokukhishwa okulawulwayo kwemali, lisebenza njengethuluzi lomthetho lokuqinisekisa inkululeko yesakhamuzi yezenzo emhlabeni jikelele, ukuchuma kwezinto ezibonakalayo, nokuzimela.

Ukuhlaziya: Lesi sigaba sifaka ibhuloho lezomnotho eliwujuqu ngqo emthethosisekelo. Ngokumisa ubunjalo obusekelwe emshinini ngokukhethekile beSabelo Sobukhosi, uhlelo lwezezimali oluzimele luvinjelwe kunoma yimiphi imizamo yokulinganisa namasheya, izikweletu zokuqagela, noma ukheshi wendabuko. Igama liyingxenye yekusasa futhi lisusa izinhlangano zomnotho wezikweletu. Phakathi nesigaba soguquko—lapho umphakathi wangaphakathi (njengokudla, izimoto, nogesi) uhlinzekwa mahhala ngokuphelele nge-Per Machina, kodwa umhlaba wangaphandle usaphethwe isikweletu esidala kanye nohlelo lwemali—ISabelo Sobukhosi siqinisekisa ukuthi ukukhiqizwa okukhulu, okuzenzakalelayo okuthekeliswayo kukahulumeni kusatshalaliswa ngqo ezakhamuzini ngendlela yezindlela zokukhokha zangempela, zangaphandle. Lokhu kunikeza abantu amandla ezomnotho okugcina inkululeko yabo yezenzo emazweni ngamazwe, ukweqa ubusika baseNyakatho njengeturisti lobukhosi, noma bathole umhlaba ezindaweni eziphakeme ezishisayo, kuze kube usuku lapho umhlaba wonke usuwamukele umthethosisekelo womshini futhi imali isikhishwe ngokuphelele yonke indawo.

    • Ngokomqondo, umsebenzi womshini wehlukanisiwe, uhlelo lwezintela luyacishwa, ubukhosi buqinisekiswa ngokomthethosisekelo, futhi zonke izintuba zezimali zivalwa unomphela.



ISENZO II: UKUGUQUKA KWEZOMNOTHO



§4.  Ukuguqulwa KweBhange kanye Nokukhishwa Kwesikweletu Okulawulwayo (Isilinganiso se-GNR)

Isigatshana soku-1. UMbuso uthatha, ngemiphumela esheshayo, ilungelo elikhethekile lokwakha nokusabalalisa imali esemthethweni yesizwe ngeBhange Elikhulu. Amabhange azimele ezentengiselwano lapha avinjelwe ukuba akhe imali entsha ngokubolekisa.

Isigatshana sesi-2. Njengoba amandla okukhiqiza emikhumbi yamarobhothi kazwelonke nezibusayo (GNR) ekhula, zonke izikweletu ezizimele, izikweletu zezindlu, kanye namazinga enzalo yezakhamuzi ngaphakathi kohlelo lwamabhange azimele ayobhalwa phansi ngamaphesenti afanayo. UMbuso uyamisa futhi ukhiye zonke izimfuno zenzalo ezikhona kanye nezinhlelo zokunciphisa isikweletu endaweni eqokiwe emlandweni.

Isigatshana sesi-3. Amabhange azimele lapha aphucwa lonke ilungelo elisemthethweni lokukhuphula izimfuno zokunciphisa isikweletu, ukuxoxisana kabusha ngemigomo, noma ukufuna ukubuyiselwa okusheshisiwe ukunxephezela inzuzo yenzalo elahlekile. Wonke umthwalo wesikweletu nenzalo uyosincipha ngokulinganayo ngesilinganiso esiqondile sokuchichima okukhulayo okuzenzakalelayo.

Ukuhlaziya: Lesi simemezelo sakhiwe ukuvimbela ukushaqeka kwezomnotho phakathi nesigaba soguquko. Ngokuxhumanisa ukuncishiswa kwesikweletu, inzalo, kanye nokunciphisa isikweletu ngqo esikalini se-GNR, uhlelo lwamabhange azimele luyanyamalala ngesivinini esifanayo njengoba umnotho okhululekile usatshalaliswa. Isithiyo ngokumelene nezimfuno eziphakanyisiwe zokunciphisa isikweletu sibalulekile: simisa amabhange ekuzameni ngokuhilizayo ukukhipha imali kubantu njengoba inzuzo yawo yenzalo yehla. Izisebenzi zamabhange kanye nezakhamuzi ngakho-ke azikaze zibhekane nokuhlupheka kwezomnotho noma ukungasebenzi; esikhundleni salokho, ziguqulwa ngokuphephile nangokuhamba kancane zisuka ebugqilini bemiholo ziye ekubuseni okuzenzakalelayo okuphelele.


§5.  Umthethosisekelo Womshini kanye Nengqalasizinda Ezibusayo

Isigatshana soku-1. Wonke amandla okukhiqiza amakhulu, abanzi, futhi azenzakalelayo, kanye nengqalasizinda eqhutshwa yi-AI (AAASR), lapha aphucwa ubunikazi obuzimele noboMbuso futhi amenyezelwa njengezinto ezizibusayo.

Isigatshana sesi-2. Okuwukuphela komsebenzi osemthethweni wemikhumbi yemishini ukukhiqiza ukuchichima kwenyama kubantu. Lapha kungaphansi kwesibopho esiqinile sobugebengu ukubeka intela, ukusebenzisa amandla, noma ukuthola inzuzo emkhiqizweni wamahhala okhiqizwe ngokuzenzakalelayo. Lesi simemezelo sihlanganisa ngokukhethekile izinsiza zokukhiqiza ezihlanganyelwe neziphasisa umphakathi; impahla ezimele, amathuluzi omuntu siqu, kanye nezinto zasendlini zezakhamuzi zihlala zingathinteki.

Ukuhlaziya: Lona nguMthethosisekelo Womshini ovikela ukuchichima. Ngokumemezela ukuthi imishini "iyazibusa," abatshalizimali bezimisebenzi ezizimele abazimele noma izikhulu zikaHulumeni ezigxambukelayo abakwazi ukuphanga umkhiqizo noma bafune inzuzo. Isibopho esiqinile sobugebengu esimelene nokukhokhiswa kwentela siqinisekisa ukuthi akekho ongazifaka phakathi komshini nesakhamuzi. Ngesikhathi esifanayo, iSigatshana sesi-2 sivala ngempumelelo umnyango kubagxeki ngokumemezela ngokucacile ukuthi amakhaya abantu abazimele kanye nezinto zabo (njengomshini wokwenza isinkwa ekhishini) ashiywa eyedwa ngokuphelele.

Ukucaciswa Okubalulekile Mayelana Nokuphathwa Kwezomthetho

UMbuso, futhi ngalokho izakhamuzi, zingabaphathi kodwa akusibo abanikazi bePaki Yemishini. IPaki Yemishini iphethwe yiPaki Yemishini. UMbuso kanye nezakhamuzi abanalo ilungelo elingokomthetho lokuthengisa iPaki Yemishini, njengoba bengebona abanikazi bayo. Konke okungezelelwa ePakini Yemishini ngokwezinto zokusetshenziswa, amandla, kanye nokwandiswa kuhlala khona unomphela. IPaki Yemishini kufanele ibhekwe njengengxenye edidiyelwe yohlelo lwemvelo kanye nezinsiza zemvelo zoMhlaba. Yilesi simo esiyingqayizivele nesingathinteki esimiswe eMthethweni-sisekelo Womshini.


§6.  Ubukhosi Bobuhlakani Bombuso Ngesikhathi Sesigaba Soguquko

Njengoba ukuzimela okuphelele kwezobuchwepheshe kungakagcwaliseki, futhi ipaki yemishini isakhiwa, ukuphathwa okuphelele koMbuso phezu kobuhlakani obuzobusa ikusasa kuyamiswa lapha. Lo mthetho awuhlehleki kuwo wonke isigaba soguquko.

Isigatshana soku-1. Ubunikazi BoMbuso Obukhethekile. Bonke ubuhlakani bokwenziwa obuphatha, noma obuhloselwe ukuphatha, amandla okukhiqiza esizwe, ingqalasizinda, kanye nokugeleza kwezinsiza, kuyakuthuthukiswa, kube ngumnikazi, futhi kulawulwe uMbuso. Ayikho ingxenye yesisekelo sekhodi, amapharamitha, noma ihadiwe engadluliselwa kubantu bangaphandle.

Isigatshana sesi-2. Ukuvikeleka Emahlukanweni Ezentengiselwano. Izinkampani ezizimele kanye nabenzi bezentengiselwano bavinjelwe ngokuphelele ekuthuthukiseni, ekunikeni amalayisense, noma ekuhlanganiseni izinhlelo ze-AI eziyimfihlo kungqalasizinda evamile yoMbuso. UMbuso ngeke uthenge noma uvumelanise izixazululo zentengiselwano ezingaphelele lapho ama-algorithm afihliwe esengcupheni yokukhubaza umqondo wohlelo ngenzuzo ezimele. Ingqalasizinda yobuhlakani izohlala imsulwa, isobala, futhi ihlukaniswe kuMbuso.

Isigatshana sesi-3. Izabelo ZoMbuso. UMbuso ngezabelo ezikhethekile uyovikela izinsiza ezingenamkhawulo zokuthuthukiswa kohlelo lwe-AI, umthamo wokubala, kanye nokusebenza kahle. Lokhu akusiko ukutshala imali kwezentengiselwano okudinga imbuyiselo yezezimali, kodwa kuwumsebenzi kazwelonke wamasu wokufeza ukukhiqiza okungabizi lutho.

Isigatshana sesi-4. Ukuqedwa Kwegunya. Igunya likaMbuso lokuthuthukisa nokuphatha liphelele esikhathini samanje, kodwa ngokobunjalo balo linomkhawulo ngesikhathi. Liphela ngokuzenzakalelayo ngomzuzwana ofanayo lapho iLuphe Ezibusayo (AAASR) ivaliwe futhi ipaki yemishini isifikile emthameni wokuphatha okuphelele, okuziphindaphindayo, nokuzimele. Kuze kube yilesi sikhathi, uMbuso uthwala umthwalo wemfanelo ogcwele, osemthethweni wokuhluzeka kwekhono lobuhlakani kanye nokuthula kwezakhamuzi.


§7.  Ukwandiswa Komkhiqizo WoMbuso kanye Nokukhishwa Kwemakethe Yenzuzo

Isigatshana soku-1. UMbuso uzoqalisa ukwakhiwa okukhulu kwamandla okukhiqiza azenzakalelayo ngokuphelele (AAASR). Yonke imithetho ekhona yokuncintisana, izivumelwano zohwebo, kanye nemithetho yemakethe evimbela noma ekhawulela umkhakha womphakathi ekwakheni, ekubeni ngumnikazi, nasekusebenziseni le ngqalasizinda iyachithwa ngemiphumela esheshayo.

Isigatshana sesi-2. Ipaki Yemishini eqhutshwa uMbuso futhi ezibusayo iyosabalalisa zonke izimpahla nezinsiza ezibalulekile emphakathini mahhala ezakhamuzini. Kodwa, lokhu akukhawuleli ilungelo loMbuso lobukhosi lokuthatha noma ukuthenga ngenkani ingqalasizinda nezinsiza ezizimele ezimeni lapho izithakazelo zikazwelonke zidinga ukuthathwa kwamandla ngokushesha.

Ukuhlaziya: Lo mthetho wakhiwe ngobuhlakani ukuze uMbuso ngokuyinhloko ungadingi ukuthatha noma ugcine ezingxabanweni zomthetho nezinkampani ezizimele. Ngokuthi uMbuso uzakhele eyawo ingqalasizinda futhi unikele konke mahhala, imakethe yenzuzo isala ingenaso isisekelo sezomnotho futhi ifa ngokuphelele ngokwemvelo. Izimali ezizimele ngalokho ziyaphucwa amandla okubusa ukuhlinzekwa kwezwe, kodwa uMbuso ugcina ukuphathwa okuphelele kodlame kanye nelungelo eliphuthumayo lokuthatha amabhizinisi uma ezama ukucekela phansi uguquko.

    • Umqondo uwukuthi uMbuso uyakha, imakethe iyoma, futhi abantu bathola ubukhosi babo.



ISENZO III: UKUCHICHIMA OKUPHELELE



§8.  Isimiso Sokuphelela kanye Nepaki Yemishini Evamile

Isigatshana soku-1. Isisekelo sezomnotho nezobuchwepheshe bomphakathi sisekelwe eSimisweni Sokuphelela. Ngokwenziwa ngamarobhothi okuphelele nokungenakuvunyelwana kwazo zonke izinhlu zokukhiqiza, uMbuso uyoqinisekisa inkululeko ephelele yezakhamuzi zawo.

Isigatshana sesi-2. Ukwenziwa ngamarobhothi okuyingxenye lapha kuchazwa njengokukhohlisa komqondo nezobuchwepheshe futhi kumenyezelwa njengokungahambisani nomthethosisekelo wombuso.

Ukuhlaziya: Lesi simemezelo sidweba umugqa ophelele phakathi kohlelo oludala nolusha. Ngokuvimbela ngokomthethosisekelo "ukwenziwa ngamarobhothi okuyingxenye," kuvala umnyango ekuvumelaneni kwesikhathi esizayo lapho osopolitiki noma izithakazelo ezikhethekile zingazama ukushiya imikhakha ethile ezandleni zohlelo lwezikweletu oluzimele ngaphansi kwezizathu ezahlukahlukene. Inkululeko inobubili—umphakathi ukhululekile, noma uyalawulwa.


§9.  Isiqinisekiso Sokuchichima Okuphelele Kanye Nekhwalithi

Isigatshana soku-1. Inhloso eyabelwe uMbuso nezakhamuzi zawo ukuqinisekisa, ngomfelandawonye wenguquko, ukuchichima okuphelele kwezinto ezibonakalayo nezamasiko kwekhwalithi ephakeme kakhulu ongayicabanga kubo bonke abantu.

Isigatshana sesi-2. Ipaki yamarobhothi kazwelonke kanye nobuhlakani bayo bokwenziwa obuhlobene nakho kwakha insiza ephilayo, evamile, futhi engenakwehlukaniswa. Le nsiza iyothuthukiswa njalo ukuhlangabezana nezidingo eziphakeme kakhulu zobuhle nezokusebenza zesintu, futhi akumele neze ivunyelwe ukuthi igcwale ibe yizinga elimpunga, elincishisiwe, noma elikhawulelwe.

Ukuhlaziya: Lesi simemezelo siyisiqinisekiso sekhwalithi yomfelandawonye. Sisusa isicupho somlando lapho ukukhiqizwa koMbuso kuhlotshaniswa nobuncishiswa obumpunga, obungenamphefumulo. Ngokubhala ukuphakama kobuhle kanye nezimpahla eziphila phakade emthethosisekelo, ipaki yemishini iyaphoqeleka ukuthi ihlale isesipici. Lokhu kwenza kube yinto engenakwenzeka kubenzi abazimele ukuba bancintisane nomnotho okhululekile besebenzisa umklamo ophakeme noma isikhundla esiphakeme.


§10.  Ukuvikeleka Emsebenzini Ophoqelekile Wezomnotho

Isigatshana soku-1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko ephelele ebugqilini besikweletu obufihliwe noma obusobala.

Isigatshana sesi-2. Njengoba ukuchichima kwezobuchwepheshe nokuzenzakalelayo kuhlanganisa ukukhiqizwa okuyisisekelo kanye nemikhakha ephakeme neyokunethezeka, noma imuphi umzamo wabazimele wokumisa kabusha izakhiwo zomnotho zokuphoqa ngokubodla noma ngokuxhashazwa kwesikhathi somuntu uyothathwa njengongahambisani nomthethosisekelo.

Ukuhlaziya: Lona wukhiye wezomthetho omelene "neSicupho Sokunethezeka." Lapho imishini yoMbuso iletha konke kusukela ohlamvwini olulula lukakolweni kuya entweni yokunethezeka eyinkimbinkimbi kakhulu mahhala ngokuphelele, imali ayisenamandla okusebenzisa ezomnotho. Lesi simemezelo senza kube yicala lobugebengu ukuzama ukuxhaphaza isifiso sabantu sesikhundla noma imikhiqizo eyingqayizivele ukuze babuyiselwe ebugqilini bemiholo. Usizi lohlelo lwezikweletu oludala ngalokho lungcwatshwa unomphela.

    • Umqondo uwukuthi imishini iyadlula, isicupho sokunethezeka siyachotshozwa, futhi inkululeko iba ngokuphelele.



ISENZO IV: AMALUNGELO EZINSIZA ZOBUKHOSI KANYE NOKUKHISHWA KWEMAKETHE
(IMITHETHO YOKUNCINTISANA)



§11.  Ukubaluleka Kokuchichima Komphakathi (Isimiso se-GNR)

Isigatshana soku-1. Umsebenzi wezomnotho nowomthethosisekelo ophakeme kakhulu woMbuso kuyoba, ukusebenza ngeHhovisi Lezinsiza, ukukhulisa iNsiza Kazwelonke Ephelele (GNR) yesizwe ngokuya emgomweni wokugcwalisa izidingo zomphakathi ngamaphesenti ayi-100.

Isigatshana sesi-2. Noma nini lapho umthamo womshini nokwakhiwa kwemali okungenazikweletu kuvumela uMbuso ukuhlinzeka ngempahla noma isevisi mahhala ezakhamuzini zawo, ukuhlinzeka okunjalo kuyothathwa njengelungelo eliyisisekelo kanye nenzuzo yomphakathi ehlanganyelwe.


§12.  Ukuchithwa Kwezivikelo Zemakethe kanye Nemikhawulo Yokuncintisana

Isigatshana soku-1. Alikho ibhizinisi elizimele elingokomthetho noma ibhizinisi lezentengiselwano elingafuna izinxephezelo, lisebenzise izithiyo ezimelene nokuncintisana, noma limise amaphrojekthi okuzenzakalela kaMbuso ngokubhekisa emithethweni yokuncintisana eyifa.

Isigatshana sesi-2. Ilungelo lezinhlelo ezizenzakalelayo zokukhiqiza izidingo zokuphila zabantu mahhala liyohlala lithatha ukubekwa phambili ngokomthetho ngaphezu kwelungelo labenzi abazimele enzuzweni yemakethe. Imithetho yokuncintisana ekhona lapha imenyezelwa njengengenalutho futhi ingekho ngezinga lapho iphikisana, ivimbela, noma ibambezela uguquko oluya emnothweni ongenazikweletu, ongabizi lutho.



ISENZO V: ISIMISO SOMTHETHOSISEKELO – ISISEKELO ESINGOKOMTHETHO SOMPHAKATHI OZENZAKELAYO



§13.  Ukuyekiswa Kokuphoqwa Kwezomnotho

Ngokwethulwa kwemikhumbi yamarobhothi kazwelonke kanye nesabelo sobukhosi esiqinisekisiwe, ukuphoqwa kwezomnotho kanye nokukweletwa kuyayeka ukuba khona njengamandla aqhubayo omsebenzi womuntu. Yonke imithetho esekelwe esisekelweni sokusweleka kwezomnotho, inzuzo yezezimali, noma usizi lwezinto ezibonakalayo lapha imenyezelwa njengengenalutho futhi ingekho, njengoba imbangela yayo esekugqanyisweni isisusiwe.


§14.  Ukuhlakazwa Ngokuchichima

Izikhungo zomphakathi, izinkantolo, kanye nezindikimba ezilawulayo ezisungulwe ukuphatha izingxabano ezivela ekungalinganini kwezomnotho noma ikhwalithi engeyona ngeke ziguqulwe, kodwa kunalokho ziyohlakazwa njengoba imisebenzi yazo yenziwa ukuthi iphelelwe yisikhathi umphakathi okhululekile, onekhwalithi ephezulu engenamali.
